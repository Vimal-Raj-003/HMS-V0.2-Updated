## 1. Purpose

TR-008 gives the trauma centre a legally robust medico-legal system: a gapless **MLC register**, automatic **police intimation** in the jurisdiction's format with proof of dispatch and acknowledgment, structured **injury documentation** on an anatomical body map with BNS classification (simple/grievous, weapon type, age of injury), **wound photography** with SHA-256 hashing, immutable storage and **chain of custody** for photos, clothing, samples and foreign bodies, evidence handover to police with signed receipts, generation of **court/injury/wound/age certificates** and §65B/BSA §63 electronic-record certificates, a **sexual-assault (SAFE) protocol** compliant with MoHFW 2014 guidelines and POCSO, **brought-dead / death** handling with inquest coordination, and MRD sealing/retrieval for summons. Care is never delayed for legal formalities.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Casualty medical officer / EM physician** (tablet in ER; desktop): confirm MLC criteria, complete MLC form (history as stated, informant, identification marks, injuries on body map, opinion on nature/weapon/age), take/annotate photos, order forensic samples, sign; issue wound certificate; attend to sexual-assault survivor with protocol; declare brought-dead and initiate inquest intimation.
- **ER nurse** (tablet): assist documentation, preserve clothing/belongings, seal evidence bags with labels, record custody transfers, chaperone for examinations, log injuries observed during nursing care.
- **Forensic medicine specialist / designated MLC officer** (desktop): review & finalise reports, age estimation with radiology/dental, prepare court reports/certified copies, respond to summons/RTI-like requests within law, testify support (case bundle).
- **MRD officer** (desktop): maintain sealed MLC records, retrieval log, certified copies (§65B/BSA §63 certificate), retention forever, court dispatch register.
- **Security officer** (desktop/phone): police liaison log (constable name/badge, GD entry no), evidence handover witness, unknown-patient identification, safety of survivor/violent patients.
- **Treating doctors/surgeons**: record intra-op forensic findings (bullet retrieval, foreign body) with custody, MLC banner awareness, discharge gating.
- **Medical Superintendent/legal**: oversight, overrides (unflag/discharge), court summons tracker (NC-023), police queries approvals.
- **Police/court** (external): receive intimation (email/portal/hard copy), acknowledgment, evidence, certified reports — never direct EMR access.
- **Patient/survivor**: informed consent for examination/photography/sample collection (with right to refuse without losing treatment), free treatment (BNSS §397), copies as per rules, counselling.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 MLC initiation & register

1. Trigger: OP-006 flag (`er.mlc.flagged`), TR-001 mechanism suggestion, IP admission flag (later discovery, e.g. assault history), OT finding, or death classification → **System** ensures **MLC number** (`MLC` gapless series per branch/year, from OP-006) and creates `mlc_cases` (linked ER visit/admission/patient or unknown tag) with category (RTA, assault, burns, poisoning, fall, industrial, animal bite, firearm, sexual assault, self-harm/attempted suicide, dowry-related, custodial, unknown/unconscious, brought dead, drowning, electrocution, snake bite (state SOP), sports/other), brought-by (self/relative/police PS/108/passer-by) with police details (PS, officer name/badge, GD/DD entry, FIR no if any), informant identity, patient identification marks (two moles/scars etc.), consent statuses → **MLC banner** appears on all screens for the patient (OP-002/IP-003) → Event `mlc.case.opened`.
2. **Register view**: gapless list per branch/year with status (open/report_pending/report_final/closed), police intimation status, key dates; register printable in the state's format; MS can unflag only with reason (audited; entry retained as "cancelled" — number never reused).

### 3.2 Police intimation & acknowledgment

1. **System** auto-generates **police intimation** (state format template EN-039: hospital name, MLC no, patient details/description, brought by, alleged history, condition, admitted/discharged, doctor name/reg no, date/time; special formats: RTA (MV Act §134 accident info to PS + insurer form), assault/grievous, poisoning, burns (dying declaration request to Magistrate/Executive Magistrate when serious), sexual assault (survivor's consent to inform police is respected for adults; POCSO mandatory), death/brought dead (inquest §174/BNSS §194)) → dispatch channels: printed copy via security to jurisdiction PS with **acknowledgment capture** (constable name/badge, signature/photo, time), **e-mail** to PS/CCTNS mailbox (`mlc.police_portal_email` — with read receipt/ack log), SMS to control room per state SOP, and record in intimation log; time from arrival to intimation KPI (target ≤ 1 h) → Event `mlc.police.intimated`.
2. **Dying declaration** request log (Magistrate call, time, arrival, whether patient fit — doctor certifies fitness "compos mentis" with time and vitals) and recording of DD by Magistrate/police (hospital only certifies fitness; copy sealed).
3. Police queries/requests (case history, injury report, blood alcohol result) → request register → provided per law (only injury report/certificates; full record on court order) with copy log; direct chart access to police never granted.

### 3.3 Injury documentation & body map

1. **CMO** documents each injury: type (abrasion, contusion, laceration, incised, stab, chop, firearm entry/exit, burn (degree/TBSA), fracture (link TR-002), bite, ligature, defence wounds), **anatomical site** on SVG body map (front/back/left/right/head/palms/soles/genital (SAFE) views; adult/child), size (L×B×D cm), shape, edges/margins, direction, distance from landmarks, colour/healing stage (age estimation of injury: fresh, 12–24 h, 2–3 days…), foreign bodies, tattooing/singeing (firearm range), **BNS classification** simple/grievous (grievous per §116: emasculation, permanent privation of sight/hearing, loss of member/joint, permanent disfiguration of head/face, fracture/dislocation, endangering life/severe pain ≥ 15 days — with dropdown reason), weapon type opinion (blunt/sharp/pointed/firearm/thermal/chemical/animal), consistent with history? → numbered on map (Injury #1…) → mirrored to TR-001 injuries when trauma episode exists (single source, dual view).
2. **Photography** (`mlc.evidence_hashing`): in-app camera (tablet/phone) with mandatory scale marker/L-rule & MLC no card in frame, overview + close-up; each image immediately hashed **SHA-256** on device, uploaded to WORM bucket (S3 object-lock), metadata (device id, user, GPS optional, timestamp server-verified) → `mlc_evidence` row type=photo with hash; annotations stored as separate layers (original never modified); consent for photography recorded (or emergency/legal exception noted); no gallery/local storage on device (PWA blocks save); viewing audited (`READ_PHI`).
3. Alcohol/intoxication assessment (smell, speech, gait, pupils, Romberg) with blood alcohol sample if consented/legally required (OP-004 custody), DOA screen (poisoning), gastric lavage sample preservation (poisoning: preserve vomitus/lavage/blood/urine in labelled sealed containers → custody), foreign bodies (bullets/pellets/glass) preserved.

### 3.4 Chain of custody & evidence handover

1. **Evidence items**: clothing (each item described, condition, cuts/stains, dried before packing in paper bags — protocol prompts), belongings/valuables (from OP-006 belongings register), samples (blood/urine/swabs/nail scrapings/hair — OP-004 samples with tamper-evident seal no), foreign bodies (bullets — never handled with metal forceps prompt), photographs/videos, documents (dying declaration copy) → each gets **evidence label/QR** (EN-013: MLC no, item no, description, sealed by, date/time), tamper-evident bag/seal number recorded.
2. **Custody log** (append-only, hash-chained): every transfer scan (from user → to user/location: ER nurse → evidence locker → lab → police) with witness, purpose, condition of seal, signatures (e-sign pad/photo); location tracking (evidence locker register with access log); temperature for biological samples.
3. **Handover to police**: request with authority (officer, PS, FIR/case no, requisition letter scan) → **System** prints handover memo (items list, seal nos, hashes for digital media, condition) → signatures both parties + witness (security) → status `handed_over` → Event `mlc.evidence.handed_over`; digital media handed over as hash-verified copies with §65B/BSA §63 certificate signed by MRD/IT custodian.
4. Retention: physical items retained until police collect (SLA per SOP; reminders), then per court; digital forever; disposal only with legal clearance (log).

### 3.5 Reports & certificates (`mlc.court_reports`)

- **Wound/injury certificate** (state format), **MLC report** (final medical opinion: nature simple/grievous, weapon, age of injuries, danger to life, fitness statements), **age estimation certificate** (physical, dental, radiological — ossification centres from OP-008 with radiologist opinion; range stated), **fitness/intoxication certificate**, **sexual-assault medical report** (MoHFW format), **death summary/inquest documents** (cause of death opinion provisional; MCCD Form 4/4A via IP-017), **discharge/treatment summary for court**, **§65B/BSA §63 electronic evidence certificate**, **certified copy issuance** (fee/no-fee rules; copy register) — all generated from structured data via EN-039 templates, e-signed with DSC (EN-016), versioned & hash-chained; corrections by addendum only; dispatch register (to court/police/patient) with acknowledgment; summons/court appearance tracker (NC-023: date, court, case no, doctor, documents needed, outcome).

### 3.6 Sexual-assault / POCSO protocol (`mlc.sexual_assault_protocol`)

1. Survivor arrives → **immediate care first**; private room; female attendant/chaperone; informed **consent** (adult; guardian for < 12 y; assent 12–18 y — child-friendly), consent granularity (examination, evidence collection, police information — adult may decline police intimation but treatment continues; POCSO minor → mandatory reporting to SJPU/local police & CWC within SOP) → **history** (survivor's words), **examination** per MoHFW proforma (general, injuries on body map incl. genital views; **two-finger test prohibited** — form omits; virginity/"habituated" language blocked), **SAFE kit** collection with checklist (swabs, clothing, nail scrapings, hair, blood/urine; each labelled/sealed → custody), emergency contraception, STI/HIV PEP (≤ 72 h), tetanus, hep-B, pregnancy test/MTP counselling (MTP Act), injury care → **psychosocial support** referral (counsellor/OP-032, one-stop centre/Sakhi, legal aid info), **safety plan**, follow-up (2 wk/6 wk/3 mo/6 mo HIV) → **Report** in MoHFW format (opinion "no injury does not rule out assault"), copies: survivor (free), police (on request/consent), sealed to court; **free treatment** (BNSS §397 / CrPC §357C — billing blocked/waived automatically) → Events `mlc.sexual_assault.case_opened`, `mlc.evidence.sealed`.
2. Access strictly limited (`mlc.sensitive_access`); no names on boards; media queries blocked; audit every view.

### 3.7 Brought-dead & death handling (`mlc.brought_dead`)

1. **Brought dead**: ER doctor examines & declares death (time, signs), no treatment charges (OP-006), MLC opened automatically (category brought_dead), external injuries documented on body map/photos, identification & belongings, **police intimation for inquest** (§174/BNSS §194), body to mortuary (IP-017) with tag & custody; **post-mortem** required → body released only to police/PM authority with handover; unknown body → police photo/description, preservation period per SOP; NOK communication support.
2. **Death of MLC patient in hospital** (ER/OT/ICU/ward): treating doctor death summary + provisional cause; unnatural death → inquest intimation, no MCCD issue until PM decision (or as per police), body handover to police for PM or to relatives with police NOC (record NOC no); natural death in MLC (e.g. old assault) → MCCD via IP-017 with MLC note; brain-death/organ donation (THOA) in MLC → police NOC before retrieval (TR-006/IP-019).
3. Death certificate & MCCD (Form 4/4A) generation lives in IP-017; TR-008 supplies MLC linkage & inquest status; **mortality register** cross-reference.

### 3.8 Special categories & edge cases

1. **Poisoning/snake bite/animal attack**: category-specific proforma (substance, quantity, time, container photo, antidote given), sample preservation (gastric lavage/blood/urine), intimation per state SOP (snake bite MLC varies by state — configurable), poison-centre contact log (OP-006).
2. **Burns**: TBSA/depth from TR-001, dying-declaration fitness prompt when TBSA > 30 % or airway burns, dowry-related suspicion checklist for married women (< 7 y marriage) → mandatory intimation & Executive Magistrate; kerosene/acid attack → Acid Attack SOP (free treatment, ₹ compensation scheme info).
3. **Custodial injury/death**: separate intimation to Magistrate/NHRC per SOP; senior doctor examination; strict access.
4. **Attempted suicide**: MHCA §115 presumption — no criminal intimation framing; MLC still recorded for unnatural injury per state practice (config); psychiatry referral mandatory (OP-032).
5. **Firearm/blast**: entry/exit wounds, tattooing, range opinion; bullets/pellets retrieved (TR-004) → custody; ballistics handover to FSL via police.
6. **Age estimation requests** (police for unaccompanied minors/juveniles): physical + dental + radiological (OP-008 ossification series) → certificate with range; JJ Act references.
7. **Drunkenness certificate** requests: consent (or legal compulsion note), clinical assessment + blood alcohol (OP-004) with custody; certificate template.
8. **Absconding/escape of custody patient**: immediate police update; security incident (NC-019/NC-015).
9. **Requests from media/insurers**: denied by default; insurers get treatment summaries via EN-002 with consent, never MLC evidence.
10. **Records for court after years**: MRD retrieval log; certified copies with §65B/BSA §63; doctor summons calendar; original evidence integrity re-verified (hash check) before production.

### 3.9 Discharge/transfer gating & closure

- Discharge/transfer/LAMA/absconding of MLC patient: minimum set required (MLC form signed, injuries documented, police intimation done, evidence sealed) else block (MS override audited); absconding → police informed (event); transfer-out → receiving hospital MLC continuity note; case closure when report final and evidence handed over/retained; MRD seals record (NC-003), any later access = break-glass with reason.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- Treatment never waits for MLC formalities or police arrival (Parmanand Katara) — UI never blocks clinical orders on MLC status; only discharge/transfer/body release are gated.
- MLC number gapless per branch/year; unflag = cancel with reason (MS only), number retained; MLC banner on every patient screen and printouts ("MLC No…").
- Police intimation must be generated within 1 h of MLC opening (KPI) and dispatched with acknowledgment capture; failed channels retried; state format templates versioned; RTA cases also generate MV Act information; dowry-related burns/injury of married woman < 7 y → mandatory intimation regardless of consent; sexual assault adult survivor may decline police information (record) — POCSO minors always reported (SJPU + CWC within 24 h SOP).
- Injury record: site, type, dimensions, classification & weapon opinion mandatory before report finalisation; classification per BNS reasons enum; consistency-with-history captured; photos need consent unless legal exception documented.
- Photos/videos: captured only via in-app camera; hashed on device before upload; uploaded to WORM storage; original immutable; annotations separate; download/print produces watermarked copies with hash & viewer identity; every view audited; export only via handover/certified-copy workflow.
- Custody log append-only with hash chain; a transfer requires both parties (2nd party PIN/e-sign or external signature capture) — no silent moves; seal-number mismatch flags incident (NC-015).
- Reports: generated from structured data; final versions immutable; addendum for corrections; DSC/e-sign mandatory for court reports (EN-016); certified copies numbered in copy register; issuance to patient/police/court per policy matrix (default: patient gets injury certificate & discharge summary; police get intimation & injury report on requisition; full records only on court order via MS/legal approval).
- Sexual-assault module: two-finger test/virginity fields do not exist; language filters block prohibited terms in free text (warn); access limited to care team + designated officers; no board/TV display; billing auto-waived (free treatment) with reason code; follow-up schedule auto-created.
- Brought dead: no treatment charges; MLC mandatory; body release only to police/PM or relatives with police NOC recorded; unknown bodies preserved per SOP with photo & description shared to police.
- Death of MLC inpatient: MCCD issuance blocked until inquest/PM decision recorded (or MS override with legal note); TR-006 brain-death organ retrieval requires police NOC record.
- Discharge/transfer/LAMA gating: minimum set = MLC form signed by doctor, ≥ 1 injury documented (or "no external injury" recorded), intimation dispatched, evidence sealed/handed; absconding → auto police update.
- Retention permanent; MRD seals record at closure; access thereafter break-glass; DPDP: law-enforcement disclosures logged with authority reference.
- Numbering: `MLC` (OP-006), `MLC_EVID` item no per case, `MLC_COPY` copy register, `MLC_REPORT` per type.

## 13. Non-functional

- Volumes: 60–120 MLC/day (trauma centre), 20 sexual-assault cases/month, 300 photos/day (≈ 1.5 GB/day WORM), 5 brought-dead/day; register 40k rows/year.
- p95: open case < 200 ms; photo hash+upload (5 MB) < 4 s on 4G; intimation PDF < 2 s; custody transfer < 200 ms; register list < 200 ms.
- Offline: body-map/injuries/photos capture offline on ER tablets (encrypted IndexedDB, hashed at capture, auto-upload; device wipe on logout); intimation generation needs network (print fallback).
- Storage: WORM bucket with object-lock (compliance mode), KMS encryption, permanent retention, cross-region replication (cloud) / off-site copy (on-prem).
- Printing: A4 statutory formats; label printer for evidence; watermark on all copies.
- Accessibility/i18n: forms bilingual (English + state language) — statutory formats as per state; RTL-ready for global.
- Security: strictest ABAC; sensitive cases hidden from boards/searches (name search returns "restricted" for non-authorised); every access audited (`READ_PHI`); no PHI in notifications; hash chain integrity job daily (alerts on tamper); legal hold flag prevents any archival move.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given OP-006 flags MLC for an RTA patient at 09:00, then a gapless MLC number exists, `mlc_cases` row is created, banner shows on OP-002/IP-003, and a police intimation draft (state format, RTA variant with MV Act info) is generated; if not dispatched by 10:00 the CMO and security receive an "intimation due" alert.
2. Given intimation dispatched by e-mail and hand copy, then dispatch log stores message id and the constable's name/badge/signature/photo with time; KPI computes arrival-to-intimation minutes.
3. Given the doctor places injury #3 (laceration 4×1×0.5 cm, right parietal, red margins, fresh) on the body map and classifies grievous (fracture reason), then the injury row mirrors to TR-001, and the wound certificate lists it with classification and weapon opinion.
4. Given a photo captured in-app, then SHA-256 is computed on device before upload, stored in WORM storage; a later attempt to delete or overwrite via API is rejected; the viewer download is watermarked with hash + user id and audited.
5. Given clothing sealed as item 2 with seal S-1183, when handed from ER nurse to evidence locker keeper, then both parties authenticate (PIN/e-sign) and the custody chain shows seq 1→2 with hash chain valid; a later transfer with seal number mismatch raises an NC-015 incident.
6. Given police requisition for evidence items 1–3, then a handover memo lists items/seals/hashes, captures officer details & signatures with security witness, sets status handed_over, and a §65B/BSA §63 certificate is generated for digital items.
7. Given the MLC report finalised and DSC-signed, then it is immutable; a correction creates an addendum version referencing the original; both appear in the copy register.
8. Given an adult sexual-assault survivor declines police information, then treatment and evidence collection proceed, police_informed = declined_adult is recorded, billing is auto-waived (free treatment), and no board/TV shows the case; a POCSO minor case forces SJPU/CWC intimation tasks within 24 h.
9. Given SAFE protocol workspace, then no field for "two-finger test" or "habituated" exists and free text containing those terms is warned/blocked before save.
10. Given a brought-dead patient, then MLC opens with category brought_dead, no treatment charges post, external injuries/photos recorded, inquest intimation generated, body custody = mortuary, and MCCD issuance is blocked until PM decision is recorded.
11. Given an MLC inpatient discharge requested with intimation not dispatched, then IP-002 shows the gate blocked with reasons; MS override requires reason and is audited.
12. Given a user without `mlc.sensitive_access` searches a sexual-assault survivor's name, then results show "restricted record" without details and the attempt is audited.
13. Given ER tablet offline, then injuries and photos are captured (hashed locally, encrypted) and upload automatically on reconnect with identical hashes; no image is stored in the device gallery.
14. Given the daily integrity job, then any hash-chain break in custody logs or evidence hashes triggers an alert to MS/IT with the affected ids.
15. Given a court summons entered with hearing date, then the doctor receives T-7/T-1 reminders and the case bundle (reports, evidence list) is retrievable by MRD.
16. Given MS cancels an MLC flag with reason, then the number stays in the register as cancelled and banners disappear; the action is audited and reported.
17. Given a burns patient with TBSA 45 %, then the dying-declaration fitness prompt appears, and if married < 7 years the dowry-suspicion checklist and Executive Magistrate intimation task are mandatory.
18. Given a bullet retrieved in OT (TR-004), then an evidence item is created from the OT specimen event, custody starts with the scrub nurse, and handover to police requires FSL requisition details.
19. Given an insurer requests MLC photographs, then the request is denied by default with a logged reason; a treatment summary is available via EN-002 only with patient consent.
20. Given a court production request 6 years later, then MRD retrieves the sealed record, hashes of evidence are re-verified and reported as intact, and certified copies with §65B/BSA §63 certificate are issued and logged in the copy register.

### RTA victims — cashless treatment entitlement (added on review)

Road-accident victims are the highest-volume MLC category in an Indian trauma centre and have a statutory funding
path that the billing flow must respect:

- **Motor Vehicles Act §162 read with the Cashless Treatment of Road Accident Victims Scheme (2025)** entitles an
  RTA victim to cashless treatment up to **₹1.5 lakh** for the golden-hour period at a designated facility.
- On `mlc.registered` with `cause = road_traffic_accident`, the system must: (a) tag the encounter payer as
  `SCHEME_RTA_CASHLESS` provisionally, (b) **suppress cash demand at every collection point** (same hard block as
  RC-007 scheme beneficiaries) until eligibility is confirmed or the ₹1.5 L cap is exhausted, (c) open an RTA claim
  case in RC-007 with the police intimation reference and vehicle details from this module, (d) alert the insurance
  desk within the configured golden-hour window, and (e) track cap consumption on the IP bill with an alert at 80 %.
- Capture, in the MLC record, the fields the claim needs: FIR/GD number, police station, accident date/time/place,
  vehicle registration(s) if known, whether the patient was driver/pillion/pedestrian, and the informant.
- If eligibility is denied, the payer reverts to the patient's own payer with a documented reason and the suppressed
  charges become collectible — never silently written off.
