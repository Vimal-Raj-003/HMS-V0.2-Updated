## 1. Purpose

IP-015 adapts the critical-care engine (IP-009) to neonates and children: incubator/warmer thermal management logs, neonatal vitals with age-banded normal ranges and PEWS/neonatal scoring, feeding chart with breast-milk/formula/TPN volumes and tolerance, weight-based everything (drugs, fluids, feeds), growth monitoring on preterm (Fenton/Intergrowth) and WHO/IAP charts, phototherapy and bilirubin nomogram management, respiratory support tracking (CPAP/HFNC/ventilator/surfactant), screening (ROP, hearing, metabolic, CCHD pulse-oximetry), Kangaroo Mother Care, and structured family communication — while feeding NICU-specific charges and outcomes to billing and quality.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Neonatologist / paediatric intensivist** (desktop/tablet): admit from labour room/ER/referral with gestational age & birth weight, set thermal/respiratory/feeding/fluid plans, review flowsheet and growth, adjust TPN/feeds daily, order surfactant/phototherapy/exchange transfusion, run rounds, discharge with follow-up plan.
- **NICU/PICU nurse** (tablet/desktop; 1:1–1:3 ratios): hourly/2-hourly vitals (auto from monitors), incubator temperature & humidity, feeding chart per feed (time, type, volume offered/taken, residuals, vomiting, stool), daily weight, head circumference weekly, phototherapy hours & eye protection checks, line care, KMC sessions, developmental care.
- **Dietician / lactation counsellor**: feed plan (EBM/DHM/formula/HMF fortification, ml/kg/day), TPN prescription with pharmacy, mother's expressing log.
- **Pharmacist**: neonatal dilutions, TPN compounding (IP-014), dose checks by weight/PMA.
- **Family** (phone via PE-001): daily update, photo (with consent), visiting/KMC slots, milk drop-off instructions.
- **Billing**: incubator/warmer/CPAP/ventilator/phototherapy day-wise charges auto from device logs.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 NICU/PICU admission & baseline

1. Source: IP-011 delivery (`newborn.registered` → newborn admission with mother link, gestational age (weeks+days), birth weight, APGAR, resuscitation, delivery mode, maternal risk factors: GDM/PIH/PROM/GBS), OP-006 ER (out-born referral: transport details, temperature on arrival, glucose, TOPS score), ward transfer, or PICU admission (age, weight, PIM3/PRISM inputs).
2. **Doctor** admits to NICU level (I/II/III) bed type (radiant warmer/incubator/cot/PICU bed) → **System** captures: corrected GA, weight class (ELBW < 1000 g / VLBW < 1500 g / LBW < 2500 g), SGA/AGA/LGA (Fenton percentile auto), admission diagnoses (ICD-10 P-codes: RDS, prematurity, sepsis, HIE, MAS, TTN, jaundice…), initial scores (**Downes/Silverman** for respiratory distress, **SNAPPE-II** at 12 h, **CRIB-II** for < 32 wk; PICU: **PIM3**, **PRISM III**, GCS-paediatric, PEWS) → plans: thermal (skin-servo/air-servo target), respiratory (room air/O2/CPAP/HFNC/ventilator/HFOV), fluids (ml/kg/day day-wise schedule; GIR mg/kg/min), feeding (NPO/trophic/advance rate), monitoring frequency, antibiotics (EOS calculator optional), vitamin K/eye care/HepB per protocol → Event `nicu.admitted`.
3. Baseline order set (OP-002 order sets by weight/GA): sepsis screen, glucose q3h, bilirubin at 24 h, cranial USG day 3/7 for < 32 wk, ROP screening due date (auto: 31 wk PMA or 4 wk of life, whichever later), hearing screen before discharge, metabolic screen 48–72 h.

### 3.2 Flowsheet, incubator/warmer log, respiratory support

1. IP-009 hourly flowsheet with neonatal parameters: HR, RR, SpO2 (pre/post-ductal), temp (skin & axillary), BP (cuff/invasive), perfusion/CRT, apnoea/bradycardia/desaturation (ABD) events count, colour, activity, glucose; age-banded normal ranges drive alerts (EN-029) rather than adult NEWS2; **PEWS** for paediatrics (age bands) with escalation ladder (IP-003).
2. **Incubator/warmer log** (`nicu_thermal_log`, hourly or per change): mode (servo skin/air/manual), set temp, measured air temp, humidity %, skin temp, warmer heater output %, phototherapy on/off, device asset id (NC-020); device feed via EN-042 (`nicu.device_feed`) auto-fills; hypo/hyperthermia alerts (< 36.5 / > 37.5 °C) → nurse task; incubator change/cleaning every 7 days task (IP-012).
3. **Respiratory**: IP-009 ventilation episodes extended: modes (nCPAP, NIPPV, HFNC, SIMV/PC/VG, HFOV — MAP/amplitude/Hz), FiO2, PEEP/PIP, surfactant doses (product, dose/kg, time, INSURE/LISA method), caffeine, blood gases (linked OP-004), extubation readiness, BPD definition at 36 wk PMA (O2 need) auto-flag; SpO2 target range per protocol (90–95 %) with alarm-limit compliance audit.
4. **Infusions**: IP-009 infusion tracking with neonatal units (ml/kg/h, mcg/kg/min), TFI (total fluid intake) auto-sum vs prescribed ml/kg/day; GIR display; UAC/UVC/PICC line days & CLABSI bundle (IP-012).

### 3.3 Feeding chart, fluids & TPN

1. **Feed plan** (doctor/dietician): type (EBM / donor human milk / preterm formula / term formula / mixed), route (breast/paladai/cup/OG/NG/gastrostomy/continuous), volume per feed & frequency (q2h/q3h), fortification (HMF), advance rate (ml/kg/day), maximum, hold criteria (residuals > 50 %, abdominal distension, bilious aspirate — NEC watch); **TPN** prescription (amino acids g/kg, lipids g/kg, dextrose %, electrolytes, trace elements, calcium/phosphate compatibility check, osmolarity, central vs peripheral) → IP-014 compounding worksheet & label; daily TPN sheet.
2. **Nurse feed entry** per feed (`nicu_feeds`): time, type, offered ml, taken ml, residual ml/colour, vomiting, abdominal girth, stool (count/type), urine (nappy weight g), tolerance flag; running totals: enteral ml/kg/day, parenteral, TFI, kcal/kg/day (auto), I/O balance (IP-003), weight-adjusted; **breast-milk tracking** (`nicu_milk_log`): mother's expressed volume, container barcode (mother UHID), fridge/freezer location & expiry (fresh 4 h RT/ 96 h fridge/ 6 mo freezer per policy), thaw time, **bedside two-identifier/barcode verification before feeding** (wrong-milk hard-stop), donor milk lot (`nicu.milk_bank`).
3. Alerts: TFI deviation > 10 % from prescribed, feed held ×2, no urine 12 h, weight loss > 10 % birth weight, hypoglycaemia protocol steps (glucose < 45 mg/dL → recheck/feed/IV bolus per EN-029 rule).

### 3.4 Growth monitoring

1. Daily weight (g, same scale, calibrated NC-020), weekly length & head circumference → plotted on **Fenton 2013 / Intergrowth-21st** (preterm, by PMA & sex) and after 40 wk (or term babies) on **WHO 2006 / IAP 2015** charts (shared with OP-033); z-scores/percentiles auto; velocity g/kg/day (rolling 7 d); flags: weight loss > 10 %, failure to regain birth weight by day 14, growth velocity < 15 g/kg/day, crossing 2 major percentiles; extrauterine growth restriction at discharge (< 10th percentile) auto-flag → dietician task.
2. PICU: WHO/IAP weight-for-age, BMI, MUAC; nutrition screening (STRONGkids/PYMS).

### 3.5 Jaundice & phototherapy

1. Bilirubin results (TSB from OP-004 / TcB device) auto-plotted on **Bhutani nomogram** (hour-specific) and AAP 2022 phototherapy/exchange thresholds by GA & risk factors (isoimmune, G6PD, asphyxia, sepsis, albumin) → recommendation banner (start/continue/stop phototherapy, exchange transfusion threshold) — doctor confirms.
2. **Phototherapy order**: units (single/double/LED/biliblanket), irradiance check (µW/cm²/nm — nurse records meter reading per shift), eye shield & genital cover checks q shift, position change, temperature/hydration monitoring, hours on/off logged (`nicu_phototherapy_log`, auto from device on/off via EN-042 or manual) → per-hour/day billing to IP-005; rebound bilirubin 12–24 h after stop auto-ordered task.
3. **Exchange transfusion**: checklist, blood component request to IP-007 (fresh, irradiated, aliquot volume double-volume calc = 2 × 80–85 ml/kg), procedure record with volumes in/out per cycle, vitals, complications.

### 3.6 Scores, sepsis, HIE & procedures

- Scores stored in `ip.icu_scores` (IP-009) with new types: SNAPPE-II, CRIB-II, Downes, Silverman, Bell staging (NEC), Sarnat (HIE) + therapeutic hypothermia protocol log (target 33.5 °C 72 h, rewarming), Thompson score, Ballard (GA assessment), NIPS/N-PASS/FLACC pain, PIM3, PRISM III, PEWS, PELOD-2 (PICU).
- Neonatal sepsis: EOS risk (Kaiser calculator inputs optional), sepsis screen results, antibiotic day counter with 48-h stop prompt if cultures negative (IP-012 stewardship).
- Procedures: intubation, UAC/UVC/PICC insertion (site, depth, X-ray confirmation), lumbar puncture, chest drain, PDA closure meds; consents from parents (EN-028).

### 3.7 Screening, KMC & developmental care

- **Screening tracker** per baby with due dates & results: ROP (dates per PMA schedule, zone/stage/plus disease, treatment laser/anti-VEGF — ophthalmologist), hearing (OAE 1/2, BERA), CCHD (pre/post-ductal SpO2 at 24 h), metabolic (TSH/G6PD/others per state), cranial USG, hip (DDH) exam, immunisation birth doses (OP-013) → discharge blocked (soft) if due screens missing.
- **KMC** (`nicu.kmc`): session log (start/end, provider mother/father, duration, vitals stability) with daily hours target; family teaching checklist (breastfeeding, danger signs, CPR basics, car seat/positioning) before discharge.
- Developmental care: noise/light bundle, positioning, non-nutritive sucking; physiotherapy referral (OP-015).

### 3.8 Family communication & discharge

1. `nicu.family_updates`: daily structured update by doctor (condition, respiratory/feeding status, plan) visible in PE-001 family view (parents' consent, mother's UHID link), optional photo, visiting/KMC slot booking (EN-015 pass), milk drop-off log; counselling notes for critical prognosis with attendee list.
2. **Discharge criteria** checklist: weight ≥ threshold & gaining, full oral feeds, thermoregulation in cot ≥ 24–48 h, no apnoea for 5–7 d off caffeine (config), screens done, immunisations, parents trained, follow-up plan (OP-033 high-risk clinic dates, ROP follow-up, hearing, physiotherapy, vaccination schedule) → IP-002 discharge summary neonatal template (birth details, course by system, discharge weight/HC, feeds, meds, follow-up) → Event `nicu.discharged`; readmission & mortality feed NNF indicators.

### 3.9 Exceptions

- Baby transferred out for surgery/other hospital → IP-018 with neonatal transport checklist (temperature, glucose, airway, lines, mother's consent, milk).
- Death → IP-002 death path, IP-017; perinatal death audit (CDR/PDR forms per state) task.
- Device feed loss → manual entry with `source=manual`; incubator failure → BME breakdown (NC-020) & bed swap.
- Multiple births: linked siblings (twin A/B) with mother; wrong-baby prevention via wristband scan (mother–baby paired bands from IP-011).

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- All drug/fluid/feed calculations use current weight (updated daily; stale > 48 h blocks weight-based order with warning) and PMA where relevant; dose-range checks neonatal-specific (EN-029) — hard-stop over max.
- Breast-milk feeding requires barcode/2-identifier verification of container vs baby (mother link); mismatch hard-stops; expired milk cannot be scanned as used.
- TFI computed = enteral + parenteral + IV meds flushes; alert if > 10 % over prescribed; TPN Ca×PO4 compatibility rule warns > product limit.
- Bilirubin recommendation is decision support only; doctor acknowledgement required for start/stop phototherapy; rebound check task auto after stop.
- Phototherapy/incubator/ventilator/CPAP charges: computed from device logs (hours→day/hour units per tariff RC-003) and posted daily to IP-005; manual gaps > 2 h need nurse confirmation.
- Screening due dates auto-computed (ROP: later of 31 wk PMA or 4 wk life for < 34 wk / < 2000 g / risk; hearing before discharge; CCHD ≥ 24 h); overdue → task; discharge soft-blocked (override with reason).
- Thermal alerts: skin temp < 36.5 or > 37.5 °C sustained 2 readings → task; incubator humidity plan by GA/day.
- Consent from parent/guardian for procedures/photos/portal sharing (EN-028); minor's data shared only to linked guardians (DPDP).
- Mother–baby band pairing verified on every KMC/feed handover; twins distinguished by band colour + suffix.
- Records append-only, versioned, hash chain (as IP-003/IP-009).

## 7. Domain Events (outbox)

- `nicu.admitted` {stay_id, admission_id, ga, bw, level} → IP-005 (NICU charges), IP-012, EN-018, PE-001 (family), OP-033 (follow-up registry).
- `nicu.thermal.alert` {stay_id, temp} / `nicu.feed.held` / `nicu.tfi.deviation` / `nicu.weight.flag` {type} / `nicu.hypoglycaemia` → IP-003 tasks, EN-037.
- `nicu.milk.verified|mismatch` → nurse, in-charge (mismatch = incident NC-015).
- `nicu.bilirubin.recommendation` {zone, action} → doctor; `nicu.phototherapy.started|stopped` {hours} → IP-005, rebound task.
- `nicu.screening.due|overdue|done` → tasks, OP-033/ophthalmology worklist.
- `nicu.tpn.ordered` → IP-014 compounding.
- `nicu.family_update.published` → PE-001 push.
- `nicu.discharged` {outcome, weight, follow_up} → IP-002, OP-033, PE-002; `nicu.death` → IP-017, perinatal audit task (NC-015).
- Consumed: `newborn.registered` (IP-011), `lab.result.final` (bilirubin/glucose/gas), `device.vitals.received|device.state` (EN-042), `ip.transferred`, `ip.discharge.initiated`.

## 8. Screens (UI)

- **NICU/PICU Unit Board** (desktop wall / EN-018 TV): bed cards (baby name "B/o mother", GA/PMA, day of life, weight, support mode, feeds ml/kg/d, lines, phototherapy, alerts, screens due); colour by acuity; live.
- **Baby Chart** (desktop 3-pane / tablet): banner (mother link, twin tag, GA/PMA, weight, corrected age, allergies), tabs Flowsheet (IP-009 grid with neonatal params, ABD event counter), Thermal, Respiratory, Feeds (grid by feed time; totals bar; `F` new feed), Growth (chart toggle Fenton/WHO), Jaundice (nomogram plot), Lines/Bundles, Screening, KMC/Family, Notes; hotkeys as IP-009.
- **Feed & Milk Station** (tablet/phone at bedside; offline queue): scan milk container → verify baby → enter volumes; big numeric pad.
- **TPN/Fluid Calculator** (desktop): weight, ml/kg/day → auto-splits, GIR, compatibility warnings, sends to pharmacy.
- **Family Update Composer** (tablet/phone) and **Family view** (PE-001).
- **Screening Tracker** (desktop) with due list per unit.
- **Indicator Dashboard** (desktop).

## 9. Integrations

- EN-042 device gateway: incubators/warmers (GE Giraffe/Dräger/Phoenix), monitors, ventilators/CPAP, phototherapy units, infusion pumps, TcB meters, scales (HL7/serial via gateway); OP-004 labs; IP-007 blood; IP-014 TPN; OP-013 immunisation; OP-033 growth chart engine shared; PE-001 family portal; NC-020 device PM.

## 10. Reports & Analytics

- NNF/NABH: admissions by GA/weight class, survival, LOS, BPD/ROP/NEC/IVH/CLABSI/VAP rates, exclusive breast-milk at discharge %, KMC hours/day, weight velocity, readmission ≤ 7 d, screening completion %, phototherapy hours, ventilator days; PICU: PIM3 SMR, PICU LOS, unplanned extubation rate.
- Perinatal death audit list; JSSK/PMJAY newborn package utilisation (RC-007).

## 11. Notifications

- Push (nurse/doctor): thermal alert, feed intolerance/hold, hypoglycaemia protocol step, weight flag, phototherapy rebound due, screening due/overdue, TPN ready, milk expiring.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a newborn registered in IP-011 at 30+2 wk, 1200 g, when admitted to NICU, then stay shows VLBW, Fenton percentile, ROP due date = 31 wk PMA/4 wk life (later), and hearing/CCHD/metabolic screens are created as due.
2. Given hourly incubator feed via EN-042 with skin temp 36.2 °C on two consecutive readings, then a thermal alert task is created and appears on the board.
3. Given a feed plan of 150 ml/kg/day EBM q2h for 1200 g, when nurse records feeds, then per-feed target 15 ml is shown, and daily summary computes enteral ml/kg/day and kcal/kg/day; if TFI exceeds prescribed by > 10 %, an alert fires.
4. Given a milk container barcode from a different mother, when scanned for the baby, then a hard-stop mismatch is shown, feed cannot be saved, and an incident is created.
5. Given TSB 14 mg/dL at 40 h in a 36-wk baby with no risk factors, when the lab result arrives, then it is plotted on the nomogram with the correct risk zone and a phototherapy recommendation banner is shown for doctor acknowledgement.
6. Given phototherapy started 08:00 and stopped 20:00 next day, then 36 hours are logged and IP-005 receives charges per tariff unit; a rebound bilirubin task is due 12–24 h later.
7. Given daily weights show loss > 10 % of birth weight, then a growth flag and dietician task are created.
8. Given TPN ordered with Ca and PO4 exceeding compatibility limits, then a warning is shown before sending to IP-014.
9. Given ROP screening overdue at discharge initiation, then discharge readiness shows a soft block requiring override reason.
10. Given a family update published with photo, when the parent opens PE-001, then only linked guardians can see it and the access is audited.
11. Given the monthly indicator MV, then survival by weight class and CLABSI per 1000 line-days match underlying records.
