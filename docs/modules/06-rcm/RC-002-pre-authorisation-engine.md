## 1. Purpose

RC-002 is the engine that turns "this patient has insurance" into "₹X is authorised until date D for room class C". It runs eligibility verification, builds the payer's pre-authorisation request (auto-filled demographics, policy, ICD-10 diagnosis, planned procedure with ROHINI/scheme code, procedure-specific clinical justification template, estimated cost from RC-008, expected LOS, document checklist), collects the doctor's signed clinical section, submits through the payer's channel (portal/email/API/NHCX), tracks every state change with the payer's reference number, manages query and enhancement rounds, propagates the approved amount and validity into IP-005 as a credit limit, handles emergency/retrospective and scheme (PMJAY TMS) variants, and measures turnaround against IRDAI and payer SLAs. EN-002 provides the payer master and the desk's home screen; RC-002 provides the machine underneath.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Insurance desk executive** (desktop, dual monitor, scanner): raise pre-auth within 30 minutes of admission, chase the payer, answer queries the same day, raise enhancements before the approved amount is exhausted, get the final authorisation before discharge. Handles 60–120 live cases at a 2000-bed hospital.
- **Treating doctor/surgeon** (phone/tablet, 60 seconds of attention): read the auto-filled clinical justification, correct it, e-sign; answer medical queries; certify LOS extension.
- **Admission desk**: at admission, sees whether the patient is cashless-eligible, which room class the policy allows, and what deposit to collect if not.
- **Ward nurse / MRD**: upload investigation reports, OT notes, implant stickers to the case checklist from a tablet camera.
- **Billing executive**: sees the approved amount as a credit limit on the running IP bill, gets alerted when consumption crosses the enhancement threshold.
- **Finance manager**: SLA dashboard (our submission TAT vs payer decision TAT), payer-wise approval ratio, escalation of overdue cases.
- **Patient/family**: status on the portal/WhatsApp, expected patient share, and what to do if pre-auth is denied.
- **TPA user (PE-008)**: sees the request, raises a query, uploads the approval letter.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Trigger & case creation

1. Triggers: `ip.admission.created` with an insurance/scheme payer; `ot.booking.created` for an elective surgery ≥ N days ahead; `op.procedure.scheduled` where the payer requires OP pre-auth; `daycare.admission.created`; manual creation by the desk (e.g. patient walks in with a policy for a planned procedure).
2. System creates the pre-auth **request** under the EN-002 `ins_cases` case: type enum(initial/enhancement/extension/final_authorisation/retrospective), payer/TPA/plan snapshot, treating doctor, provisional diagnosis, planned procedure, and starts the **SLA clock**: `submit_due_at` = admission + 30 min (elective) / + 24 h (emergency, `preauth.emergency_retro`), `decision_due_at` = submitted + payer SLA (default from empanelment; IRDAI reference 1 h shown as a separate timer) → Event `preauth.case.created`.
3. **Eligibility check** (if not already done at registration): manual (call/portal, outcome recorded) or API/NHCX `CoverageEligibilityRequest` via RC-001 → returns active/inactive, remaining sum insured, room-rent entitlement, co-pay %, deductible, waiting-period and pre-existing-disease flags, network status (cashless/PPN/reimbursement-only) → written to the case and shown on the admission banner → Event `preauth.eligibility.checked`. An `inactive`/`invalid` result immediately raises a counselling task (convert to self-pay, collect deposit).

### 3.2 Estimate & request assembly

1. **Estimate**: RC-002 calls RC-008 for a payer-aware estimate (room rent × expected LOS by class, ICU days, OT & anaesthesia, implants (TR-003 with UDI and invoice value), investigations, pharmacy & consumables, professional fees, non-medical/non-payable items separated) → `requested_amount`. The estimate id is stored so estimate-vs-approved-vs-actual can be measured later (§10).
2. **Clinical justification template by procedure** (EN-039): the system picks the template mapped to the procedure/ICD group — e.g. _total knee replacement_ (duration of symptoms, failed conservative management, X-ray grade Kellgren–Lawrence, deformity, walking distance), _PTCA_ (angiogram findings, vessel & % stenosis, TIMI flow, indication), _LSCS_ (indication code, previous sections, foetal status), _cataract_ (BCVA, cataract grade), _spine fusion_ (neuro deficit, MRI findings, conservative therapy trial), _appendicectomy_ (Alvarado score, USG findings) — auto-filled from the clinical record (OP-002/IP-003 notes, OP-004 labs, OP-008 imaging reports) with the doctor editing free text. Fields the payer commonly rejects on (date of first symptom/consultation, alcohol/smoking history, injury details & MLC for accidents, previous claim history) are mandatory.
3. **Document checklist** driven by the payer template + procedure: photo ID, policy/TPA card, doctor's admission note, indoor case papers, investigation reports supporting the diagnosis, previous consultation records for chronic conditions, MLC/FIR copy for RTA, employer/ESIC certificate, consent, implant proforma for implant cases. Each item flags `required` / `received` / `verified`, with drag-drop and tablet camera capture (auto-deskew, PDF/A). Missing required documents block submission (override only by the desk lead with a reason, audited).
4. **Doctor e-sign** on the clinical section (EN-016 Aadhaar eSign/DSC or PIN + audit). Submission is disabled until signed, with the reason shown.
5. **Consent**: submission is blocked unless an `insurance_sharing` consent purpose exists for this patient (EN-028); the desk can capture it on the spot (paper scan or e-sign).

### 3.3 Submission & channels

1. Channel resolved from the empanelment: **portal** (desk uploads manually, records portal reference no and screenshot), **email** (EN-032, payer template PDF + attachments, optionally password-protected ZIP for DPDP), **payer API** (EN-017 adapter, `preauth.api_channel`), **NHCX/HCX** (`preauth.nhcx` — RC-001 builds the FHIR `Claim` with `use = preauthorization`, `CoverageEligibilityRequest` beforehand, and receives `ClaimResponse`).
2. On submission: request PDF snapshot stored with sha256, `submitted_at` recorded, payer reference captured (API/NHCX return it; portal/email entered by the desk with a nag task until entered), status → `submitted`, decision SLA timer starts → Event `preauth.submitted`.
3. Channel failure (portal down, email bounce, API 5xx) → request queued with retry/backoff and a DLQ; the desk sees "delivery failed" and can switch channels without recreating the request (all attempts logged in `preauth_transmissions`).

### 3.4 Tracking, queries and decisions

1. **State machine** (single source of truth; every transition records actor, timestamp, payer ref, letter/document, reason):
   `draft → pending_signature → ready → submitted → query_raised ⇄ query_replied → approved | partially_approved | denied | cancelled | expired | withdrawn`
   with `approved → enhancement_requested → (enhancement_approved | enhancement_partially_approved | enhancement_denied) → approved(updated total)` and `approved → final_authorisation_requested → final_authorised | final_short_authorised`.
   Illegal transitions are rejected at the service layer (a state table, not `if` chains).
2. **Query round**: payer raises a query (received by email/portal/PE-008/API) → logged with text, attachments, category enum(medical/document/policy/billing/other), routed to doctor or desk with its own SLA (payer default 24 h; ours: reply within 4 working hours) → reply with documents → `query_replied`, decision clock resumes → Event `preauth.query.raised|replied`. Query count per case is a KPI (RC-004 root cause).
3. **Decision capture**: approved amount, approved room class/entitlement, validity (`valid_till` — commonly 7/15 days for planned admissions), co-pay %, deductible, non-payable list from the payer, remarks, approval letter file → status set → Event `preauth.approved|partially_approved|denied`.
4. **Denial**: reason captured against the RC-004 taxonomy (payer wording mapped to our code) → options offered on screen: appeal/representation (RC-004 workflow), resubmit with more documents, convert to self-pay (counselling record + deposit via NC-001 + patient-informed acknowledgement), or reimbursement route (patient pays, hospital issues the claim pack). Patient is notified with the revised estimate.

### 3.5 Credit-limit propagation (to IP-005)

1. On `preauth.approved`, RC-002 emits `preauth.credit_limit.set` {admission_id, approved_amount, valid_till, room_class, co_pay_pct, deductible, non_payables[]} → IP-005 sets `credit_limit` on the running bill and switches the payer tariff (RC-003), and IP-001 shows the payer chip and approved-vs-consumed gauge on the bed board.
2. **Consumption monitoring**: a job (and the `ip.bill.updated` event) computes `consumed / approved`; at the configured threshold (default 80 %, per payer overridable) an **enhancement task** is auto-created with a pre-filled interim bill and updated clinical note (`preauth.auto_enhancement`) → Event `preauth.enhancement.due`. At 100 % the bill shows a hard warning and further credit posting requires desk acknowledgement.
3. **Room-class breach**: if the patient occupies a class above the approved entitlement, the system computes the **proportionate deduction** exposure and raises a patient-consent task (EN-002 §3.9) — the number is shown to the family at the time of upgrade, not at discharge.
4. **Validity expiry**: `valid_till` passing without admission (planned cases) → status `expired`, desk task to re-submit; for admitted patients, an LOS **extension** request is raised instead.

### 3.6 Enhancement & extension

- Triggered by: consumption threshold, LOS extension beyond approved days, change/addition of procedure, ICU transfer, implant addition, complication. The request reuses the same case (`type=enhancement|extension`), carries the interim bill (IP-005), updated clinical notes, new investigation reports, and references the original payer approval number. The approved total is cumulative (`approved_amount_total`), and the credit limit is re-propagated. Enhancement requests above a configured amount need internal approval (EN-038) before they leave the hospital.

### 3.7 Final authorisation at discharge

- On `ip.discharge.initiated`, RC-002 raises the **final authorisation** request with the final bill draft, signed discharge summary, all reports, implant invoices/stickers, and the patient-signed claim form. The IRDAI 3-hour timer is displayed with escalation at 2 hours (desk lead → finance head → payer escalation contact from the empanelment). The payer's final approved amount is written back, the patient share is computed by EN-002/IP-005, and the case hands over to RC-001 for claim submission → Event `preauth.final_authorised`.
- **Discharge is never blocked by payer delay** beyond the configured grace: after grace, the case can be released "on hospital risk" with EN-038 approval, and the claim proceeds post-discharge (NABH patient-rights rule).

### 3.8 Emergency & retrospective (`preauth.emergency_retro`)

- Emergency admission (OP-006/TR-001): treatment starts immediately; case flagged `emergency`; deposit rule per hospital policy; pre-auth must be submitted within 24 hours (payer contractual rule) with an emergency certificate from the treating doctor. A separate SLA and a distinct worklist tab exist so these are never lost over a weekend. Retrospective/regularisation requests (CGHS/ECHS emergency, PMJAY within permitted window) follow the same path with `type=retrospective` and the scheme's own time limit from RC-007.

### 3.9 Scheme pre-auth (`preauth.scheme_tms`, with RC-007)

- PMJAY: beneficiary verification → package selection from HBP → **TMS pre-auth** (blocked/unblocked packages, specialist checks, mandatory documents & photographs) → NHA approval → RC-002 mirrors the state so the desk has one worklist. CGHS/ECHS prior permission and ESIC referral follow the same state machine with scheme-specific forms and time limits. Scheme cases carry `no_patient_collection = true`, which hard-blocks patient-share collection in NC-001/OP-005/IP-005.

### 3.10 Exceptions

- Policy found invalid after approval (payer withdraws) → status `withdrawn`, credit limit revoked, counselling task, deposit collection.
- Duplicate requests for the same encounter/payer are prevented by an idempotency key (encounter_id, payer_id, type, sequence).
- Patient discharged against medical advice / death → case type variant with the appropriate document set (DAMA form / death summary).
- Multi-payer: the primary payer's decision must be recorded before a secondary request is submitted.
- Offline: the desk needs connectivity for payer channels; document capture on tablets queues locally and uploads on reconnect.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- **Submission gates** (all hard, override only by desk lead with reason + audit): doctor e-signature present; all `required` documents `received`; `insurance_sharing` consent recorded; eligibility not `not_eligible`; requested amount > 0 and ≤ remaining sum insured (warn, not block, when SI is unknown); policy valid on the date of admission.
- **SLA defaults** (configurable per payer from the empanelment): submit within 30 min of admission (elective) / 24 h (emergency); reply to a query within 4 working hours; final authorisation request within 60 min of bill finalisation. Payer-side reference timers: IRDAI 1 h for cashless authorisation, 3 h for final discharge authorisation — displayed and used for the payer TAT report, escalated at 60 % and 90 % of the window.
- Approved amount is cumulative across enhancements (`approved_amount_total`); a claim (RC-001) may not exceed it plus the configured tolerance (default ₹0 for scheme, 5 % or ₹5,000 for insurers) without an enhancement.
- **Credit limit** in IP-005 = `approved_amount_total − patient-share components` and is revoked automatically on `denied`/`withdrawn`/`expired`.
- Enhancement auto-trigger at the configured consumption threshold; the task cannot be dismissed without a reason.
- Scheme cases (`no_patient_collection`) hard-block any patient collection except statutorily permitted items; attempts are logged as compliance incidents (RC-007).
- Number series `PREAUTH` is per branch per FY, gapless not required (not a financial document) but unique.
- A submitted request is **immutable**; corrections create a new sequence referencing the parent; the PDF snapshot and its sha256 are retained for dispute.
- Every state change requires an actor and, for payer-driven states, a payer reference or a decision letter; "approved" without an amount is rejected.
- Segregation: the desk user who submits may record the payer's decision (they receive it), but a **write-off** or "release on hospital risk" decision requires a different, higher role (EN-038).
- Data minimisation (DPDP): only documents on the payer's checklist are packed; psychiatry/HIV/fertility records require an explicit additional consent tick before inclusion.
- Retrospective requests beyond the payer's/scheme's time limit are flagged `late` and require finance approval before submission (they will likely be denied and must be tracked).

## 7. Domain Events (outbox)

- `preauth.case.created`, `preauth.eligibility.checked` {eligible, si_remaining, room_entitlement} → IP-001 banner, OP-005/IP-005 tariff switch, PE-001.
- `preauth.submitted` {request_id, channel, amount, payer_ref} → EN-002 desk, PE-001/EN-009 patient message, RC-001 (NHCX correlation).
- `preauth.query.raised|replied` → EN-037 (doctor/desk task), PE-008.
- `preauth.approved|partially_approved|denied` {amount, valid_till, room_class, reason_code} → **IP-005 credit limit**, IP-001 bed board chip, RC-004 (on denial), PE-001, EN-009 patient WhatsApp, NC-001 (deposit rule on denial).
- `preauth.credit_limit.set|revoked` → IP-005.
- `preauth.enhancement.due` {consumed_pct, admission_id} → desk task, doctor notification, IP-005 banner.
- `preauth.enhancement.requested|approved|denied`.
- `preauth.final_authorised|final_short_authorised` → IP-002 discharge gate, RC-001 claim creation, NC-001 patient-share collection.
- `preauth.expired|withdrawn|cancelled` → IP-005 credit revoke, desk task.
- `preauth.sla.breached` {stage, minutes_over, level} → EN-037 escalation ladder, finance dashboard.
- Consumes: `ip.admission.created`, `ip.transferred` (ICU → enhancement), `ip.bill.updated` (consumption), `ot.booking.created`, `ip.discharge.initiated`, `insurance.policy.verified` (EN-002), `implant.used` (TR-003 → enhancement), `claim.rejected` (RC-004 feedback into pre-auth quality rules).

## 8. Screens

- **Pre-auth Worklist** (desktop, dual monitor): tabs — _To submit (SLA)_, _Awaiting decision_, _Queries open_, _Enhancements due_, _Final auth pending_, _Emergency 24 h_, _Expiring approvals_, _Denied — action needed_. Columns: patient (banner mini), ward/bed, payer/TPA, doctor, requested, approved, consumed %, SLA countdown chip (green/amber/red), last action age. ## 9. Integrations

- **Payer channels**: portals (manual, with reference capture and screenshot attachment), email (EN-032, template + attachments, optional encrypted ZIP), payer REST/SOAP APIs (EN-017 adapters with retry/backoff and DLQ), **NHCX/HCX** via RC-001 (FHIR R4 `CoverageEligibilityRequest/Response`, `Claim{use=preauthorization}`, `ClaimResponse`, `Communication` for queries), fax/courier as a recorded fallback.
- **PMJAY TMS / CGHS / ECHS / ESIC** portals via RC-007 (some are manual-only; the state machine mirrors them so the desk has one worklist).
- **RC-008** estimate, **RC-003** payer tariff, **TR-003** implant data, **IP-005** interim bills & credit limit, **IP-002** discharge summary, **NC-003** MRD scans, **EN-016** e-sign, **EN-039** forms, **EN-038** approvals/escalation, **EN-037/EN-009/EN-032** notifications, **NC-004** document store, **EN-028** consent, **EN-024** audit.
- Failure handling: every outbound attempt is recorded in `preauth_transmissions`; a channel outage does not lose the request (queued); inbound payer webhooks are signature-verified and idempotent by payer reference.

## 10. Reports & Analytics

- **TAT**: admission → request created → submitted; submitted → payer decision (vs payer SLA and IRDAI reference); query round-trip time; final-auth request → decision. By payer, TPA, doctor, department, shift.
- **Outcome**: approval ratio, average approved / requested %, partial-approval rate, denial rate with reasons (RC-004), enhancement frequency and success, expiry rate of unused approvals.
- **Accuracy**: estimate vs approved vs final bill vs settled (the four-way variance that predicts short payments) — feeds RC-008's learning loop and RC-004's preventive rules.
- **Operations**: open cases by age, SLA breaches by cause (our delay vs payer delay vs doctor signature delay — the last is the most common and needs naming), documents most often missing, top query reasons.
- Read models: `analytics.mv_preauth_tat`, `mv_preauth_outcomes`, `mv_preauth_open_aging`, `mv_estimate_vs_approved` — refreshed every 15 min.

## 13. Non-functional

- **Volumes** (2000 beds): ~150 admissions/day, ~60 % payer-funded → 90 initial pre-auths/day, ~40 enhancements/day, ~60 queries/day, 250–350 live cases at any time; 30k requests/year. NHCX message volume ≈ 4× request count (eligibility, claim, response, communication).
- **Performance**: worklist p95 < 200 ms for 500 open cases with SLA computation (SLA due times are stored columns, not computed in the query); form render to PDF < 5 s; document upload direct to S3 presigned (no API body); decision webhook processed < 1 s; SLA scanner job runs every 60 s over open cases only.
- **Reliability**: submissions are durable — an API/portal outage queues rather than fails; DLQ visible to IT (EN-017); no request may be silently lost (a daily reconciliation job compares `submitted` requests with transmissions and payer acknowledgements).
- **Offline**: tablet document capture queues in IndexedDB and uploads on reconnect; doctor signature can be queued offline and applies on sync with the original timestamp recorded.
- **Printing**: payer form (A4, payer letterhead template), document checklist, approval letter, patient estimate & patient-share sheet.
- **Accessibility/i18n**: payer forms are in English (payer requirement); the UI, patient messages and the patient-share sheet are multilingual; WCAG 2.2 AA; the worklist is fully keyboard-operable.
- **Security**: clinical documents are PHI — encrypted at rest, presigned time-limited URLs, read-audited; payer portal users see only their own payer's data (ABAC + row filter, verified by test); no PHI in notification bodies beyond first name and case reference.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an admission with an insurance payer, when the admission is saved, then a pre-auth request in `draft` exists with `submit_due_at = admission + 30 min` and appears on the worklist within 5 s.
2. Given a request whose clinical section is unsigned, when the desk clicks Submit, then submission is blocked and the tooltip names the missing signature; after the doctor signs from the phone card, Submit becomes enabled within 5 s (real-time).
3. Given a payer template requiring an ID proof and a policy card, when either is missing, then Submit is blocked; a desk lead with `preauth.override.submit_gate` may proceed with a mandatory reason, and the override is written to the audit log.
4. Given no `insurance_sharing` consent for the patient, when submitting, then the request is blocked with a prompt to capture consent, and capturing it unblocks submission.
5. Given a submitted request, when the payer approves ₹1,00,000 valid till 25-Aug for a semi-private room, then IP-005's credit limit becomes ₹1,00,000 within 5 s, the bed board shows the payer chip, and the patient receives a WhatsApp status message within 2 minutes.
6. Given an approved amount of ₹1,00,000, when the running bill reaches ₹80,000, then an enhancement task with a pre-filled interim bill is created automatically and the doctor and desk are notified.
7. Given an emergency admission at 23:00 Saturday, then the case appears in the _Emergency 24 h_ tab with a 24-hour submission timer, and escalation fires at 18 hours if still unsubmitted.
8. Given a payer query is received, when it is categorised as `medical`, then it is routed to the treating doctor with a 4-hour internal SLA, and the payer decision clock is paused from `query_raised` to `query_replied` in the TAT report.
9. Given an approval that expires without admission, then the status becomes `expired`, the credit limit is revoked, and a re-submission task is created.
10. Given a denied pre-auth, then the denial reason is stored against an RC-004 code, the patient is notified with the revised estimate, a counselling/deposit task is created, and the case offers appeal / resubmit / self-pay / reimbursement actions.
11. Given a PMJAY scheme case, when a cashier attempts to collect any patient share, then collection is blocked with "Scheme beneficiary — no cash collection" and a compliance incident is logged.
12. Given a discharge initiated at 10:00 with final authorisation requested at 10:15, then the 3-hour IRDAI timer is displayed, escalation fires at 12:15, and at the configured grace the discharge may be released on hospital risk only with Finance approval.
13. Given a payer API returns 503 three times, then the request is queued to the DLQ, the desk sees "delivery failed — switch channel", and switching to email submits the same request without creating a new one.
14. Given a duplicate create call with the same (encounter, payer, type, sequence), then only one request exists (idempotency) and the second call returns the first.
15. Given a submitted request, when anyone attempts to edit its form fields, then a 409 is returned and the UI offers to create the next sequence instead.
16. Given a TPA portal user, when listing requests, then only their payer's requests are visible and a direct API call for another payer's request returns 404.
17. Given 350 open cases, when the worklist loads with SLA chips, then p95 < 200 ms and the SLA colour matches the stored due times exactly.
18. Given a request, when the case is exported or its documents are opened, then a PHI read-audit entry is written with user, time and reason where applicable.
