## 1. Purpose

NC-031 is the **system of record for every agreement** the hospital signs — vendor supply/rate contracts (legal doc), AMC/CMC, outsourced services (housekeeping, security, laundry, canteen, FM, BMW), leases & rentals (premises, equipment, pharmacies/canteen concessions), consultant/visiting doctor & retainer agreements, corporate/TPA/insurer/scheme empanelments, referral & partner MoUs, software/SaaS licences, marketing agencies, construction/turnkey projects, research/CSR/donor agreements — with **templates & clause library**, drafting → negotiation (redlines/versions) → approvals → **e-sign/e-stamp** → active management of **obligations, milestones, deliverables, SLAs & penalties**, renewals/expiry alerts (auto-renew/notice periods), amendments/addenda, payment/guarantee schedules (NC-009), performance & compliance monitoring (with NC-021 scores and operational modules), disputes/termination (NC-023) and a searchable repository (OCR) with access control. Commercial rate lines and payouts stay in NC-021/NC-034; NC-031 supplies the governing terms.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Contracts officer** (desktop): intake requests, choose template, draft/negotiate, route approvals, execute e-sign/stamp, register metadata & obligations, monitor expiries/renewals, amendments, audits, reports.
- **Department contract managers** (owners): raise contract requests, review drafts, track vendor deliverables/milestones, record SLA breaches/penalties, approve invoices against contract (via NC-005/NC-009), initiate renewal/termination.
- **Purchase/HR/BD/Admin**: type-specific flows (vendor, people, corporate/TPA, service).
- **Finance**: payment schedules, retention/BG/SD tracking, revenue-share receivables, TDS mapping, budget commitments.
- **Legal (NC-023)**: clause approvals, deviations, disputes, litigation link.
- **Signatories** (Director/Admin): review & e-sign on mobile/desktop.
- **Counterparties** (`contracts.counterparty_portal`): receive drafts, sign, upload compliance docs (PF/ESI challans, licences), view obligations.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Contract request & drafting

1. **Requester** raises contract request: type enum(vendor_supply/rate_contract/amc_cmc/service_outsourcing/lease_in/lease_out_concession/consultant_doctor/retainer/employment_special/corporate_client/tpa_insurer/govt_scheme/referral_partner_mou/software_saas/marketing_agency/construction_project/research_csr_donation/nda/other), counterparty (NC-021 vendor / NC-012 corporate / EN-002 payer / NC-010 person / new), purpose, value & currency, term (start/end/notice), payment terms, SLA/deliverables outline, budget line (NC-022), risk flags (PHI processing → DPDP DPA annex; access to premises → security; construction → statutory), template selection (`contracts.clause_library`: standard templates per type with mandatory clauses: confidentiality, DPDP data protection, indemnity, insurance, termination, dispute/arbitration, anti-bribery, compliance with laws (contractor labour), audit rights, NMC compliance for doctor/referral agreements — no fee-for-referral) → **Draft** generated (EN-039 doc template + clause library, variables filled) → Event `contract.requested`.
2. **Negotiation**: versions with redline compare (docx/PDF diff via NC-004), counterparty draft upload/exchange (email/portal), deviations from standard clauses flagged → legal review (NC-023) required for non-standard clauses/value above threshold; risk score.

### 3.2 Approvals, stamping & signing (`contracts.esign`)

1. **Approval matrix** (EN-038) by type/value/risk (Dept head → Purchase/HR/BD head → Finance → Legal → Director/Board for high value or related-party (§188)) → approved final → **stamp duty** determination (state, instrument type; e-stamp certificate procured & attached; or franking) → **signature**: Aadhaar eSign/DSC via EN-016 for internal signatories & counterparty (portal/link with OTP), or wet-ink scan upload with witness details → **executed** copy stored (NC-004, hash) → metadata extracted (OCR/AI later) → status `active` → Event `contract.executed`.

### 3.3 Obligations, milestones, SLA & penalties (`contracts.obligations`)

1. On execution, **obligations register** created from template/entered: recurring (monthly compliance docs from contractor: PF/ESI challans, wage register, licences; insurance renewals; reports), milestones/deliverables (construction phases; software go-live; equipment installation → NC-002 capitalisation), SLA metrics (source module: NC-018 turnover SLA, NC-019 post occupancy, NC-025 WO SLA, NC-002/NC-020 uptime/response, NC-017 TAT, NC-033 hygiene, NC-021 OTD/quality) with **penalty/incentive formulas**, payment schedule (advance/milestone/monthly; retention %; BG/SD with expiry), price escalation clauses, notice periods, exclusivity/non-compete, insurance certificates → owners & due dates → tasks & alerts → evidence uploads → status → **penalty computation** monthly from SLA data → deduction advice to NC-005 invoice verification/NC-009 (or credit note) → counterparty dispute → resolution → Event `contract.obligation.due|completed|breached`, `contract.penalty.computed`.

### 3.4 Renewals, amendments, termination

- Expiry/notice alerts (180/120/90/60/30 days by type; auto-renew clauses flagged with opt-out deadline) → renewal decision workflow (performance review from NC-021 scores/SLA history → renegotiate/renew/re-tender NC-005/terminate) → renewal creates new version linked; **amendments/addenda** (scope/value/term change) with approvals & signing; **termination** (for convenience/cause) with notice tracking, exit obligations (data return/deletion per DPDP, asset handover, final settlement, BG release), dispute escalation to NC-023 → Event `contract.renewed|amended|terminated`.

### 3.5 Contract-linked spend & receivables control

- POs (NC-005) reference contract → cumulative spend vs contract value (cap alerts at 80/100 %); invoices verified against milestones/SLA (NC-009 AP hold if obligations breached); revenue contracts (concessions, lease-out, corporate credit, revenue share) → receivable schedules & escalation (NC-009 AR); BG/SD register with expiry & renewal reminders; retention releases on defect-liability end.

### 3.6 Repository & compliance

- Full-text/OCR search, filters (type/counterparty/owner/status/expiry/value), access control (confidential: HR/doctor agreements restricted), audit trail; related-party register (§188), contract calendar; contractor statutory compliance tracker (Contract Labour Act: licence, wages, PF/ESI monthly, safety) — non-compliance flags to payments; DPDP processor register (contracts processing personal data with clauses & sub-processors).

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- Every active contract must have: executed document (hash), counterparty linked to master (or justified external), owner, dates (or evergreen flag), value type, at least one obligation (renewal/notice at minimum), stamp record where legally required (state/instrument matrix; warn if missing), TDS/GST classification (finance), DPDP DPA annex if processes personal data.
- Mandatory clauses per template cannot be removed without legal approval; deviations tracked; doctor/referral agreements scanned for prohibited referral-fee language → hard block until legal override with rationale (NMC compliance).
- Approval matrix by type/value/risk/related-party; signatory authority (delegation of authority matrix) enforced; related-party contracts require §188 board approval evidence.
- Signing: all parties signed → `active`; partially signed > 30 days → alert; wet-ink requires scanned copy + witness fields.
- Obligations: due tasks to owners; evidence required where flagged; SLA metrics pulled from source modules monthly; penalties computed by formula, subject to owner review & counterparty dispute window (e.g. 15 days) before settlement advice; NC-009/NC-005 hold invoice approval when critical obligations breached (config).
- Renewals: alerts by type-specific lead; auto-renew contracts show opt-out deadline; renewal requires performance review record; expired contracts block new POs (NC-005) unless grace approved.
- Spend cap: PO issuance against contract blocked when cumulative > value (or > 110 % with approval); rate-based contracts track estimated vs actual.
- Guarantees: expiry alerts 60/30 days; release requires obligation completion (defect liability) & finance approval.
- Contractor compliance docs missing for period → payment flag; Contract Labour licence expiry → service module notified (NC-018/NC-019/NC-025).
- Numbering `CTR` per hospital/FY (+ amendment suffix); executed docs immutable; audit on all changes; confidentiality enforced in search results.

## 13. Non-functional

- Volumes: 1–3k contracts, 10k obligations/year, 200 SLA computations/month; search p95 < 200 ms (FTS + OCR index); redline generation < 10 s (worker); e-sign round-trip async with webhooks.
- Security: restricted ACLs, encrypted docs, presigned URLs, audit of views for restricted; RLS; DoA enforcement.
- Offline: n/a; mobile signing/approvals; printing executed copies with hash footer; i18n; WCAG 2.2 AA.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a housekeeping outsourcing contract request of ₹1.2 Cr/yr, then the template pre-loads mandatory clauses (contract labour compliance, DPDP, indemnity, SLA/penalty) and the approval route includes Admin → Finance → Legal → Director.
2. Given a drafter removes the DPDP clause, then submission is blocked until legal approves the deviation with rationale.
3. Given a visiting-doctor agreement draft containing "commission per referred patient", then the system blocks with an NMC compliance error; legal override requires documented rationale (audited).
4. Given approvals complete, when sent for eSign, then hospital signatory & counterparty sign via EN-016; on both signatures the contract becomes `active`, executed PDF stored with SHA-256, and NC-021/NC-002 links update.
5. Given SLA metric "bed turnover ≤ 30 min in 90 % of tasks" fed from NC-018 with July actual 82 %, then penalty computes per formula, owner reviews, vendor can dispute within 15 days, and settlement advice goes to NC-005 invoice verification.
6. Given contract value ₹50 L with POs totalling ₹48 L, when a new PO of ₹5 L is raised, then NC-005 receives cap-exceeded and requires approval; at 80 % the owner was alerted.
7. Given a contract expiring in 90 days with auto-renew and 60-day opt-out notice, then the owner gets an alert with opt-out deadline; renewal decision requires a performance review record.
8. Given a contractor's PF challan for June missing by 15 July, then a compliance flag raises and NC-009 shows payment hold recommendation for that vendor's July invoice.
9. Given a bank guarantee expiring in 30 days, then finance is alerted; release is blocked until defect-liability obligation completes.
10. Given a user without ACL for a restricted HR contract, then it is absent from search results and direct access is denied (audited).
11. Given a related-party flag, then activation requires §188 approval evidence attached and the contract appears in the related-party register.
