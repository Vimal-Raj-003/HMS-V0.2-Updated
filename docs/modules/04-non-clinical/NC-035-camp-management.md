## 1. Purpose

NC-035 plans and runs **outreach camps** — free/paid health check camps, disease screening (diabetes/hypertension/cancer/eye/dental/ortho/ENT/paediatric/school), vaccination drives, blood donation camps, corporate on-site wellness — from planning (venue, permissions, staff, kits, vehicles, budget, promotion) through **mobile offline registration** (lite demographics, consent, ABHA optional, QR slip), **on-site workflow** (vitals, screening questionnaires, doctor lite-consult, POC tests, samples/labels for lab, referrals/advice, medicine handouts), **post-camp sync** into the HMS (patients → MPI dedupe, results delivery via WhatsApp/SMS), **follow-up & conversion tracking** (referred → appointment → visit → procedure) with NC-026/PE-002, sponsor/corporate reporting, and **camp P&L** (cost vs revenue/conversions). Blood donation camps integrate with IP-007.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Camp coordinator** (desktop + phone): create camp, checklist (permissions NC-023, venue, staff NC-030, kits NC-006, vehicles NC-024, promotion NC-026), day-of dashboard (registrations, throughput), post-camp reconciliation (kits/meds/BMW), follow-up plan, reports for sponsors.
- **Registration staff** (tablet/phone offline): register participants in < 45 s (name, age/sex, phone, locality, consent; photo optional; ABHA scan/create when online), print/show QR slip, dedupe within camp.
- **Nurse/technician** (tablet): vitals (BP/SpO2/glucose/BMI via OP-007 lite), screening forms (e.g. IDRS diabetes risk, vision chart, oral screening), POC tests, sample collection with barcode.
- **Camp doctor** (tablet): lite consult (complaint, findings, provisional dx/ICD optional, advice), referral to hospital department (urgency), free medicine handout list, follow-up date.
- **Blood bank staff** (`camps.blood_donation`): donor registration/screening/deferral, bag collection, transport log → IP-007.
- **Call centre/CRM**: post-camp follow-ups (leads), appointment conversion, report delivery queries.
- **Corporate/sponsor** (portal read): headcount, aggregate health indices (de-identified), invoices (NC-012).

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Camp planning & approvals

1. **Coordinator** creates camp: type enum(general_health/screening_specific/vaccination/blood_donation/corporate_wellness/school/eye/dental/ortho/women_health/geriatric/telemedicine_outreach/awareness_only), date/time, venue (address/geo, indoor/outdoor), organiser/partner (NGO/corporate/panchayat/school — NC-021/NC-012), expected footfall, target services (packages of screening tests, consults, vaccines), pricing (free/subsidised/paid per head; corporate contract), staff plan (doctors by specialty, nurses, techs, registration, pharmacy, drivers — NC-030 roster; NC-029 geo-site), kits (consumables/medicines/POC strips/vaccines cold-chain/BP apparatus/glucometers/vision charts/ECG — NC-006 issue with return), equipment (NC-002/NC-020), vehicles (NC-024/NC-013 ambulance standby for large camps), promotion plan (NC-026 campaign, posters, WhatsApp invites with consent), budget (NC-022) & sponsor income, **compliance checklist** (health dept intimation/permission NC-023, doctor registrations, PNDT restriction acknowledgement if USG, blood camp permission from blood bank licence authority, drug dispensing licence coverage, BMW arrangement NC-016, emergency plan) → approvals (EN-038: marketing head, MS for clinical staffing, finance for budget) → status `approved` → Event `camp.scheduled`.

### 3.2 Pre-camp

- Participant pre-registration (web/WhatsApp link with slot; corporate employee list upload from PE-006), reminders (EN-009), staff briefing, kit packing list & issue (NC-006 lot/expiry check — no near-expiry meds), device provisioning (tablets with offline app & camp config), printing (slips, forms, consent), ABHA readiness.

### 3.3 Camp day — offline-first app (`camps.offline_app`)

1. **Registration**: capture minimal demographics + phone (OTP optional when online), consent (DPDP treatment & data processing; separate marketing opt-in), ABHA (verify/create if online; else later), guardian for minors, photo (optional) → local id + **QR slip** (thermal/Bluetooth printer or on-screen) → in-camp dedupe (name+phone/age) → Event (queued) `camp.registration.created`.
2. **Stations**: vitals & anthropometry (device integration BLE BP/glucometer optional via EN-042), screening questionnaires (templated per camp type; risk scores auto e.g. IDRS, Framingham lite, PHQ-2, vision acuity), POC tests (RBS, Hb, HbA1c POC, lipid POC, urine dipstick), **samples** for lab (barcode labels pre-printed; batch manifest with cold chain), ECG (device PDF), doctor consult (findings, provisional dx, advice, medicines handed (from camp stock with lot; free-medicine register), **referral** to hospital dept with urgency & suggested package/appointment, follow-up date), vaccination (OP-013 flow), dental/eye specific forms → **participant summary slip** (findings, advice, appointment info, hospital contact) printed/WhatsApp when online.
3. **Blood donation** (`camps.blood_donation`): donor form, screening (Hb, BP, weight, deferral criteria per SBTC), bag number assignment (IP-007 series reserved offline), collection time, adverse reactions, refreshments, transport temperature log & bank receipt → IP-007 sync.
4. Emergencies: referral to ER (OP-006 pre-alert), ambulance (NC-013); incident log.
5. Live dashboard when connectivity: registrations, station throughput, queue; sync status per device.

### 3.4 Post-camp sync, MPI & results

1. Devices sync (idempotent per record id) → **MPI dedupe** (OP-001 rules: phone/name/DOB) → existing patient link or **camp-lite record** (flag `camp_registered`, converts to full UHID on hospital visit with data carry-over & consent) → ABHA link where captured → lab samples received & processed under camp order (batch billing to camp/corporate; free); results → participants via WhatsApp/SMS secure link (PE-001 lite/report link) & printed batches for distribution; abnormal/critical results → doctor review & priority follow-up call (PE-002) → Event `camp.results.delivered`.
2. **Reconciliation**: kit returns/consumption (NC-006), free medicines register (lot-wise), BMW quantities (NC-016), staff attendance (NC-029), vehicle trips (NC-024), expenses (NC-009) & sponsor invoice (NC-012) → camp closed.

### 3.5 Follow-up & conversion

- Referred participants → **leads** in NC-026 (source camp) & PE-002 recall lists (call/WhatsApp with consent) → appointment booking (OP-001; camp discount code via NC-026 coupons if policy) → visit → procedure/admission → **conversion funnel** per camp (registered → screened → referred → contacted → appointment → visited → treated) & revenue attribution (OP-005/IP-005) → camp ROI; clinical follow-up quality (abnormal results contacted within 48 h %).

### 3.6 Corporate & school camps (`camps.corporate`, `camps.school`)

- Corporate: employee list import, per-head package (OP-014), on-site health index aggregate report (de-identified, DPDP), individual reports to employees, invoice per contract (NC-012), utilisation for PE-006; school: student lists with guardian consent, RBSK-style screening forms, referral letters to parents, aggregate to school.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- Camp cannot go `live` without compliance checklist mandatory items (health-dept intimation where required, MS approval of clinical staff, emergency plan, drug licence coverage for dispensing, BMW arrangement) and at least one registered doctor for clinical camps; USG use blocked unless PNDT registration & Form F workflow (OP-008) enabled for that machine/venue.
- Registration minimal fields: name, age/sex, phone (or guardian phone), consent; minors require guardian consent; marketing consent separate from treatment/data consent (DPDP); OTP verification optional (offline skips, flagged).
- Offline app: client-generated UUIDv7 ids, idempotent sync, conflict rule = server keeps first record and links duplicates; time from device clock with drift correction; data encrypted at rest on device; auto-purge after successful sync + 7 days; app requires login token valid ≤ 72 h.
- Free medicines: only from camp-issued lots (no expired/near-expiry ≤ 3 months), doctor attribution mandatory; Schedule H/H1 as per prescription; narcotics prohibited at camps; register printable.
- Blood donation: SBTC donor criteria enforced (age 18–65, weight ≥ 45 kg, Hb ≥ 12.5 g/dL, interval rules); bag numbers from IP-007 reserved series; transport to bank ≤ 6 h with temperature; sync mandatory before bank processing.
- Samples: pre-printed barcodes mapped to registration; batch manifest reconciled at lab (missing/haemolysed flagged); results delivered only to verified phone/OTP link (or printed on request); critical results → doctor call within 24 h logged.
- MPI: match rules per OP-001; auto-merge only on strong match (phone + name + DOB); else `manual_review`; camp-lite records excluded from clinical MPI search unless flag shown; conversion to UHID copies consent & camp encounter to timeline.
- Follow-up: referrals & abnormal results must have owner & due (48 h for abnormal); contact attempts logged; conversion attribution to camp (NC-026 first-touch); coupons per policy; NMC: no inducements/commission to camp doctors per referral (NC-034 fixed camp fee only).
- Corporate camps: per-head billing on registrations/attendance per contract; aggregate reports de-identified (k-anonymity ≥ 10).
- Numbering `CAMP`, registration local_seq per camp; audit; PHI export restricted.

## 13. Non-functional

- Volumes: 5–20 camps/month, 200–3,000 registrations per camp (mega camps 10k), 10–30 devices per camp; offline app registration < 45 s; sync of 5k records < 5 min on 4G; MPI matching < 200 ms/record (batch); results delivery batch (2k) < 10 min.
- Offline: full functionality without network; local encrypted store; conflict rules; printing via Bluetooth; battery/low-end devices support.
- Security/privacy: consent-first; PII encrypted; device tokens scoped to camp; auto-purge on device; RLS; audit; DPDP purpose limitation & retention; k-anonymity for sponsor reports.
- i18n (participant languages incl. voice prompts), WCAG 2.2 AA; outdoor readability.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a diabetes screening camp draft without health-dept intimation evidence and no doctor assigned, then "go-live" is blocked listing missing checklist items.
2. Given the offline app on a tablet with no network, then registration, vitals, RBS entry, consult and QR slip printing work; on reconnect all records sync idempotently (re-sync produces no duplicates).
3. Given two registrations at the camp with same phone+name+age, then the second is flagged duplicate-in-camp and can be merged locally.
4. Given a participant with RBS 320 mg/dL, then the encounter is marked abnormal, a priority follow-up (48 h) is created on sync, and after doctor review the participant receives a call within SLA (logged).
5. Given samples for 300 participants dispatched in a batch, then the lab receives the manifest, 298 match barcodes and 2 are flagged missing; results for verified phones are delivered via WhatsApp OTP link and delivery status tracked.
