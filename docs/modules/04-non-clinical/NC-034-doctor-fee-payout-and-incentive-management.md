## 1. Purpose

NC-034 computes and pays what the hospital owes doctors and approved partners for services rendered — accurately, transparently and **lawfully**. It holds **payout models** per doctor/agreement (employed with incentives; visiting/consultant fee-share by service (consultation, procedure, surgery, visits, reporting) with % / fixed / slab / on-billed vs on-collected/realised; retainer + share; minimum guarantee; revenue-share for departments; locum per-shift; on-call allowances; teleconsult), applies **rules by service group, payer type (cash/insurance/scheme/corporate), discount-bearing policy, package splits (surgeon/anaesthetist/assistant), multi-doctor procedures, deductions (equipment usage, marketing, room), TDS 194J**, and produces **period payout statements** with drill-down to bill items, approvals, dispute handling, journal & payment (NC-009) or payroll lines (NC-010), Form 16A/26Q data, and analytics (doctor productivity/yield, cost of professional fees vs revenue). **Referral/partner payouts** are supported **only** for lawful, agreement-backed, non-clinician-influencing arrangements (e.g. corporate/wellness partners, lawful non-medical agents, empanelled diagnostic centres for logistics) with a hard **NMC anti-kickback guard**: no per-referral payment to any registered medical practitioner for patient referrals; every rule flagged `referral` requires legal attestation and is excluded from doctor-facing screens.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Payout accountant** (desktop): configure agreements/rules (with approvals), run monthly computation, review exceptions (unattributed items, disputes, negative balances), approve statements, generate TDS & journals, execute payment run/payroll hand-off, answer doctor queries.
- **Doctor** (OP-019/PE-007/web `payout.doctor_portal`): view own earnings live (billed/collected/expected), monthly statement PDF, dispute a line (wrong attribution/rate), download Form 16A (via NC-009), submit lower-deduction certificate.
- **Medical administration/HOD**: approve incentive schemes, verify surgeon/anaesthetist splits, adjudicate disputes on attribution.
- **Billing**: correct doctor attribution on items (before statement lock), package split entry at OT.
- **Payroll**: consume approved incentive lines for employed doctors.
- **Compliance/Legal**: attest referral/partner rules; audit anti-kickback controls; related-party disclosures.
- **Management**: doctor productivity, professional fee ratio, department profitability (with NC-008).

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Agreements, models & rules

1. **Accountant** creates **doctor payout agreement** (linked NC-031 contract; NC-010 employee/consultant record): model enum(employed_fixed/employed_with_incentive/visiting_fee_share/retainer_plus_share/revenue_share/locum_per_shift/on_call_only/teleconsult_only/external_reporting), validity, PAN/bank/TDS section (194J default; salary for employed), lower-deduction cert (197), payment cycle (monthly/fortnightly), payout basis enum(on_billed/on_collected/on_realised) with payer-specific overrides (e.g. cash on collected; insurance on realised after claim settlement — `payout.on_realisation`), **rule lines**: scope (service group/service/package/department/visit type/OT role (surgeon/anaesthetist/assistant/co-surgeon)/reporting (radiology modality/lab section)), payer type (self/insurance/TPA/corporate/scheme (PMJAY/CGHS/ECHS)/international), rate type enum(pct_of_net/pct_of_professional_component/fixed_per_unit/slab_by_volume/slab_by_revenue), value(s), **discount policy** enum(doctor_bears_pro_rata/hospital_bears/shared_pct/none_above_x%), free/charity cases (no payout or fixed), package split table (e.g. surgery package: surgeon 25 %, anaesthetist 8 %, assistant 3 % of package price or of professional component per RC-003 tariff), caps/floors, minimum guarantee/month with recovery rules, retainer amount, deductions (equipment usage %, OPD room rent, marketing contribution, staff cost share — configurable), on-call allowance per duty (NC-030 log), locum per shift rate, teleconsult %, camp consult fixed (NC-035), multi-doctor split rules (equal/manual per item), effective dates → **approval** (EN-038: MS + Finance head; Director above thresholds; Legal for `referral_partner` rules) → active version → Event `payout.agreement.activated`.
2. **Incentive schemes** for employed doctors/departments/staff (`payout.staff_incentives`): targets (revenue/units/quality gates e.g. documentation completeness, discharge TAT, NPS ≥ x), tiers, team pools, quality guard (no incentives on volumes of investigations ordered — policy flag), computed monthly → payroll lines (NC-010) after HOD/Finance approval.
3. **Partner/referral payouts** (`payout.referral_partner`): counterparty types allowed enum(corporate_wellness_partner/lawful_agent_non_medical/diagnostic_logistics_partner/marketing_affiliate/international_facilitator) — **not** RMP referrers for patient referrals; rule creation requires legal attestation, agreement id, GST/TDS 194H, statement visibility restricted to finance; guard: counterparty linked to a doctor registration → blocked; OP-021 referral records are analytics-only for this purpose.

### 3.2 Attribution capture (upstream, continuous)

- OP-005/IP-005 bill items carry `performing_doctor_id`, `referring_doctor_id` (clinical, informational), `ordering_doctor_id`, department, payer, package linkage, OT case roles (IP-006: surgeon/anaesthetist/assistants with split %), reporting doctor (OP-004 validated by pathologist; OP-008 radiologist signed), consult type (new/follow-up/tele/camp), collected amount & date (receipts), realisation (EN-002 settlements & disallowances per item), credit notes/refunds → NC-034 ingests events into **payout ledger lines** (pending until basis satisfied) → unattributed items (missing doctor) → exception queue to billing/OT for correction before cut-off; attribution changes after statement lock → adjustment in next period.

### 3.3 Computation run

1. Cut-off (e.g. 3rd of month for previous month; realisation items included as they settle) → **System** computes per agreement: eligible items (basis met), rule match (most specific wins: service+payer > service group+payer > service group > department; effective-dated), amount = f(rate, base (net after discount per discount policy; professional component when tariff splits), splits, caps), slabs on cumulative volume/revenue in period, minimum guarantee top-up or recovery, retainer, on-call/locum lines (NC-030/NC-029), deductions, adjustments (prior disputes, reversals from credit notes/refunds/claim disallowances → negative lines with reason), incentive schemes → **gross payout** → TDS (194J 10 %/2 % where applicable; 206AB check; LDC; threshold) → net → **draft statement** per doctor with line-level drill-down → exceptions report (negative net, missing PAN, no bank, unattributed items, rule gaps) → Event `payout.run.computed`.
2. **Review & approval**: accountant review → HOD/MS spot checks (top variances vs last month) → Finance approval → statements **published** to doctors (portal/OP-019/email PDF, e-signed EN-016 optional) → dispute window (e.g. 7 days) → disputes resolved (attribution correction/rate fix/reject) → adjustments → **lock** → journal (NC-009: Dr Professional fees expense by department/cost centre; Cr Doctor payable; Cr TDS payable) → payment run (bank file/UPI via NC-009) or payroll lines (NC-010 employed) → payment advice & Form 16A (quarterly via NC-009 26Q) → Event `payout.statement.published|locked|paid`.

### 3.4 Disputes & adjustments

- Doctor raises dispute on line (wrong doctor, missing procedure, rate) → routed to billing/OT/finance → resolution with evidence (bill, OT note) → approved correction → adjustment line in current/next period; SLA & audit; recurring attribution errors → process CAPA (NC-015).

### 3.5 Advances, guarantees & recoveries

- Advance payments/minimum guarantee → recovery schedule from future payouts; negative balances carried forward; exit/F&F for consultants (NC-010) → final statement & no-dues; retainer proration for leave/hours shortfall (NC-029 sessions).

### 3.6 Reporting & doctor visibility

- Doctor earnings widget (own only; billed vs collected vs paid; pending realisation), statement history; management: professional fee ratio, doctor yield per OPD hour, top procedures, payout by payer type, realisation lag, cost of doctors per department (NC-008/NC-022), scheme viability (PMJAY package vs doctor share).

### 3.7 Worked examples & computation order (normative)

- **Order of operations per ledger line**: (1) determine base per rule (`gross` | `net_after_discount` | `professional_component` from RC-003 tariff | `package_price`); (2) apply discount policy adjustment (doctor bears pro-rata: base × (net/gross); hospital bears: base = gross; shared: base = gross − discount × doctor_share%; `none_above_x%`: doctor bears only up to x % discount); (3) apply payer-specific overrides (e.g. scheme package flat fee); (4) apply rate (pct/fixed/slab — slabs computed on cumulative period volume/revenue at run time, re-evaluated if items are added later in the same open period); (5) apply caps/floors; (6) apply multi-doctor split; (7) mark basis eligibility (billed/collected/realised, pro-rata to receipts/settlements); (8) reversals mirror the original line with negative amounts referencing `reversal_of_id`.
- **Example A (OPD, collected basis)**: consult ₹1,000, approved discount ₹100, doctor bears pro-rata, rate 60 % → base ₹900 → ₹540 eligible when ₹900 collected; partial collection ₹450 → ₹270 eligible now, remainder when collected.
- **Example B (insurance realised basis)**: procedure billed ₹50,000, claim settled ₹42,000 (₹8,000 disallowed) → base ₹42,000 × 30 % = ₹12,600; TPA TDS on hospital's receivable does not reduce doctor base unless agreement says.
- **Example C (slab)**: 0–₹5 L revenue @ 40 %, ₹5–10 L @ 45 %, > ₹10 L @ 50 % (marginal) → month revenue ₹12 L → ₹2 L + ₹2.25 L + ₹1 L = ₹5.25 L.
- **Example D (retainer + share + MG)**: retainer ₹1,00,000 + 20 % share = ₹80,000; MG ₹2,00,000 → top-up ₹20,000 recoverable from next 3 months where share exceeds MG by that much.
- **Example E (OT roles)**: package ₹2,00,000; professional component ₹80,000; surgeon 60 % = ₹48,000, anaesthetist 25 % = ₹20,000, assistant 15 % = ₹12,000; if second surgeon added at OT with manual 40/20 split of surgeon share → ₹32,000/₹16,000.
- **Example F (TDS)**: gross ₹3,20,000 (194J) → TDS 10 % ₹32,000; with LDC at 5 % valid → ₹16,000; 206AB non-filer → 20 %.
- **Rounding**: line amounts to paise; statement totals rounded to rupee per Income-tax rounding for TDS (§288B) — configurable.
- **Cut-off & late events**: receipts/settlements arriving after lock post to the next open period; never mutate locked statements.

### 3.8 Compliance controls (summary)

- Rule-save validators: (a) no `referring_doctor_id`-keyed base for RMPs; (b) partner agreements require legal attestation & lawful counterparty type; (c) rates > 100 % or negative blocked; (d) related-party doctors flagged for §188; (e) incentive schemes cannot include ordered-investigation volume metrics unless quality-justified (policy flag + MS approval); monthly compliance report to Legal; violation attempts audited & alerted.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- **Anti-kickback guard (hard)**: no rule may pay any doctor (RMP) an amount keyed to `referring_doctor_id` of another patient encounter; `is_referral_partner` agreements cannot reference a doctor id or an entity flagged as RMP/clinic-with-RMP without legal attestation override that is itself blocked for per-referral bases (only lawful fixed-fee service contracts allowed, e.g. corporate wellness administration) — validation at rule save + monthly compliance report; UI banner cites NMC 2002 §6.4/2023 regs; clinicians never see referral analytics tied to money.
- Payout basis: on_billed (at bill finalize), on_collected (receipts pro-rata to items; partial collections proportional), on_realised (claim settlement per item; disallowances reduce base; TPA TDS/GST not doctor's burden unless agreement says) — reversals on refunds/credit notes/claim rejections create negative lines; negative net carried forward.
- Discount policy applies only to approved discounts; unauthorised discount → hospital bears (audit); package splits sum ≤ 100 % of professional component; multi-doctor items require split = 100 %.
- Rule precedence deterministic; overlapping rules with same specificity blocked; effective-dating; retro changes only via approved adjustment runs.
- TDS: 194J at 10 % (2 % where applicable to technical services per law/config), threshold & 206AB/206CCA checks (PAN status from NC-009/NC-021 verification), LDC within validity/limit; employed doctors → no TDS here (payroll §192); TDS deposited/26Q via NC-009; Form 16A shared to doctor portal.
- Statement lifecycle: computed → published (dispute window) → locked (immutable; corrections via adjustments) → journal → paid; payment only after Finance approval & lock; SoD: rule creator ≠ approver ≠ payer; doctor cannot edit own rules.
- Minimum guarantee: top-up when share < MG; recovery per rule from future months up to cap; retainer prorated by NC-029 sessions if agreement says.
- Unattributed items at cut-off: excluded with exception list; when corrected later → next period adjustment.
- Doctor visibility: own data only; earnings widget shows pending realisation separately; no patient identifiers beyond bill no./date/service (masked name optional per policy).
- Numbering `PAYOUT_STMT` per doctor/period; audit trail; DPDP: earnings restricted data class.

- Offline: n/a; printing/PDF statements with e-sign; i18n; WCAG 2.2 AA.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given Dr A (visiting, 60 % of net consultation fee on collected basis) with a ₹1,000 consult (₹100 approved discount, doctor bears pro-rata) collected on 5 Aug, then the ledger line becomes eligible on 5 Aug with base ₹900 and computed ₹540 in the August run.
2. Given the same consult under insurance with `on_realised` basis, then the line stays pending until the claim settles; settlement of ₹800 (₹100 disallowed) yields base ₹800 → ₹480; a later claim rejection reverses it.
3. Given a surgery package ₹2,00,000 with professional component ₹80,000 and splits surgeon 60 %/anaesthetist 25 %/assistant 15 %, then three lines compute ₹48,000/₹20,000/₹12,000; if IP-006 roles record two surgeons with manual 40/20, lines follow the case roles.
4. Given a rule draft paying Dr B ₹500 for each patient where Dr B is `referring_doctor_id`, then saving is blocked with the NMC anti-kickback message and a `payout.compliance.violation_attempt` event is logged.
5. Given a partner agreement for a corporate wellness administrator with fixed monthly fee and legal attestation, then it can be activated; a per-referral rate on the same partner is blocked.
6. Given Dr C's August gross ₹3,20,000 with valid PAN and no LDC, then TDS 194J = ₹32,000, net ₹2,88,000; with 206AB non-filer status TDS is at the higher rate and flagged.
7. Given a statement published on 5 Sep with 7-day dispute window, when Dr C disputes a line on 8 Sep for wrong attribution and billing corrects it, then an adjustment line appears and the statement locks after resolution; payment occurs only after lock and finance approval.
8. Given minimum guarantee ₹2,00,000 and computed share ₹1,50,000, then a ₹50,000 MG top-up line is added and recovery is applied from future months per rule.
9. Given an unattributed OT consumable-included procedure at cut-off, then it appears in exceptions, is excluded from the run, and after correction posts to the next period.
10. Given a credit note issued in September for an August paid item, then a negative line appears in September's statement (carried forward if net negative).
11. Given a doctor logs into OP-019, then they see only their own earnings and statements; attempts to query another doctor's statement return 403 and are audited.
12. Given the run is locked, then NC-009 receives one journal per department cost centre and a payment batch; totals reconcile with statements.
13. Given an employed doctor with an incentive scheme gated on discharge summary completion ≥ 90 %, when actual is 85 %, then incentive is zero for the period per gate rule and HOD sees the reason.
14. Given slab rule 40/45/50 % and month revenue ₹12 L, then computed share = ₹5.25 L (marginal slabs) and adding a late ₹50k item before lock recomputes slabs consistently.
15. Given a partial collection of ₹450 on a ₹900 base consult (collected basis), then ₹270 is eligible now and the remaining ₹270 becomes eligible when the balance is collected; the doctor's widget shows pending ₹270.
16. Given a receipt reversed after the August statement locked, then a negative line appears in September, and August remains immutable with the reversal reference visible.
17. Given an incentive scheme draft including "number of CT scans ordered" as a metric, then saving is blocked unless the quality-justification flag is set and MS approves; the attempt is logged in the compliance report.
18. Given a related-party doctor (director's spouse) agreement, then activation requires §188 approval evidence and the payout appears in the related-party report.
