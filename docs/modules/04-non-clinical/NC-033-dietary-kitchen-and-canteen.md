## 1. Purpose

NC-033 runs the hospital's food service: converts **IP diet orders** (from OP-011/IP-003) into meal-wise production counts and **tray-line tickets** per ward/bed with therapeutic diet, texture, allergens and NPO handling; **menu planning** (cycle menus by diet type/season/veg-non-veg with recipes, nutrient values, cost), **production planning** and recipe-based raw material consumption (kitchen store NC-006 with auto-indent), **meal dispatch** by ward with time/temperature and receipt/feedback; **canteen POS** for staff/visitors/attendants (menu master, tokens/coupons, cash/UPI/card/salary deduction, staff subsidy per grade, GST), **hygiene & HACCP** logs (temperatures, cleaning, pest control, food handler health), **food waste tracking** with menu optimisation, vendor-managed/outsourced catering SLA, and reports (cost per meal/patient-day, revenue, waste, hygiene score). Nutritional assessment/diet prescription stays in OP-011; NC-033 executes.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Diet kitchen supervisor** (desktop/tablet): see today's meal-wise counts by diet type & ward, allergen/NPO changes in real time, print tray tickets/labels, manage tray-line, dispatch carts with time stamps, handle late orders/changes; verify therapeutic diet compliance.
- **Chef/kitchen manager**: plan cycle menus & recipes with dietician approval, production sheets, raw material requisitions, staffing, equipment, HACCP compliance, waste review, cost control.
- **Ward nurse** (IP-003 widget/tablet): confirm NPO/diet changes flow, receive cart, mark tray delivered/returned, record intake % & patient feedback (with OP-011), request late tray.
- **Dietician**: approve menus/recipes for therapeutic diets, review dispatch exceptions, feedback analytics.
- **Canteen cashier** (POS touchscreen): fast billing (menu tiles/barcode), tokens, staff ID scan for subsidy, UPI QR, day-end (NC-001).
- **Staff/visitors** (app `dietary.online_ordering`): pre-order meals, wallet/tokens, pickup slots; patients' attendants order paid meals to bed.
- **HR/Finance**: subsidy config, monthly salary deduction feed, GST, revenue, cost per meal.
- **Quality/ICN**: hygiene audits, temperature logs, food handler health, complaints.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Diet order integration → meal list (`dietary.patient_meals`)

1. **System** consumes `diet.ip_order.created|changed|stopped` (OP-011/IP-003 order sets), `ip.admitted|transferred|discharged`, `ot.case.scheduled` (NPO), `patient.allergy.recorded` → maintains **active diet per bed** {patient (name, UHID last 4, bed), diet type code (normal/soft/liquid clear/full/diabetic/renal/cardiac/low-salt/high-protein/bland/tube-feed formula & rate/paediatric/maternity/religious-veg-jain-halal preferences), texture (IDDSI 0–7), portion, allergens, dislikes/preferences, calorie/protein target, NPO from–to, extra items (juice, snacks), attendant meal (paid), special instructions} → **meal cut-off** times per meal (breakfast/mid-morning/lunch/evening/dinner/bedtime; e.g. lunch cut-off 10:00) → **counts** by ward × diet type × texture generated for production; late changes after cut-off → "late tray" queue with supervisor acknowledgement → Event `dietary.meal_list.generated|changed`.
2. Special diets (diabetic/renal/soft/liquid) prepared per menu variants; allergen cross-check at ticket printing (allergen ↔ recipe ingredients) → hard warning; NPO → no tray + ward informed.

### 3.2 Menu planning & recipes

- **Cycle menus** (7/14/21-day) per diet type & meal with recipes (ingredients, quantities per portion, method, allergens, nutrients per portion computed from IFCT 2017 food composition (OP-011 shared table), cost per portion from NC-006 rates), seasonal/festival variants, veg/non-veg/egg flags, religious options → **dietician approval** for therapeutic diets → menu of the day auto-derived; canteen menu master (items, category, prices, tax, availability slots, combos) → Event `dietary.menu.published`.

### 3.3 Production, tray-line & dispatch

1. Production sheet per meal (counts × recipe → quantities; batch cooking; **HACCP CCPs**: cooking core temp, hot-holding temp before dispatch, cold items ≤ 5 °C) → **tray tickets/labels** (EN-013/EN-005: ward, bed, patient name + UHID last 4, diet type/texture, allergens in red, items, time) — printed per ward in dispatch order → tray assembly with checklist (scan ticket → tick items) → **cart dispatch** per ward with time stamp & temperature probe log → ward receipt (nurse scans cart/ticket) → tray delivered per bed (nurse/GDA tick or scan) → **intake %** & patient feedback (smiley + comments) captured (feeds OP-011 `meal.delivered`) → tray return & waste (plate waste category) → exceptions: patient shifted (re-route), NPO now (hold), refused, wrong diet (incident) → Event `dietary.meal.dispatched|delivered|refused`.
2. Late/extra trays: nurse request → supervisor approve → produce → dispatch; attendant paid meals delivered with patient tray (charged to IP-005 or POS).
3. Outsourced catering (`dietary.outsourced`): vendor receives meal counts/tickets via portal, delivers with temperature logs, SLA (on-time %, temperature compliance, complaints), billing per meal by category (NC-005/NC-009).

### 3.4 Canteen POS, tokens & subsidy (`dietary.canteen_pos`, `dietary.staff_subsidy`)

1. **Counter POS**: item tiles/barcode, quantity, combos, discounts (rules), customer type enum(staff/visitor/attendant/doctor/vendor_staff/guest) → staff identification (ID card scan/biometric via NC-029/EN-020 or employee code + PIN) → **subsidy** per grade/category (e.g. ₹30 per lunch, or 50 % up to cap; meals per day limit; night-shift meal free) auto-applied → net payable by cash/UPI QR (EN-010)/card/prepaid wallet/**salary deduction** (with monthly cap & consent) → receipt (thermal, GST invoice with FSSAI no.) → tokens (printed/QR) → kitchen order display (KDS/EN-018) for prepared-to-order items → collections roll into NC-001 counter shift & day-end → Event `dietary.pos.sale`.
2. **Tokens/coupons**: prepaid token books/QR coupons (departments buying for staff/events; NC-025 room booking refreshments), guest meal coupons; validation at counter (single use).
3. **Online ordering** (`dietary.online_ordering`, NC-014/PE-001): staff pre-order with pickup slot; attendants order to bed (charged to POS/wallet or IP bill with consent); menu of the day; nutrition labels (calories) at counter/app.
4. Month-end: staff subsidy report & **salary deduction file** to NC-010 (per employee: meals, subsidy, net deduction; perquisite valuation data), GST summary to NC-009, revenue by counter/category.

### 3.5 Kitchen inventory & costing (`dietary.kitchen_inventory`)

- Kitchen store as NC-006 sub-store: raw materials with FEFO for perishables (milk, vegetables, meat) & daily receiving checks (temperature, quality, vendor), **recipe-based consumption** auto-computed from production counts (theoretical) vs actual issue → variance; **auto-indent** to central stores/purchase (NC-005) from menu plan & par levels; vendor-managed perishables option (market); cost per meal by diet type; kitchen cost centre (NC-008); patient meal cost per patient-day; canteen COGS & margin.

### 3.6 Hygiene, HACCP & food safety (`dietary.haccp`)

- Daily checklists: personal hygiene, cleaning schedules (with NC-018), equipment sanitation, **temperature logs** (fridges/freezers/cold room via EN-042 sensors or manual 3× daily; cooking/holding probes), water testing (NC-025), pest control log & vendor visits, food sample retention (48–72 h) log, **food handler medical fitness** (annual; typhoid/hep A vaccination per policy — NC-010/IP-012) & FoSTaC training (NC-027), FSSAI licence display (NC-023), internal audits/scores (Quality), CAPA (NC-015); non-conformance → hold food batch; kitchen CCTV compliance (market; NC-019).

### 3.7 Waste tracking & optimisation

- Weigh/record waste by category (production over-run, plate waste by ward/diet, spoilage/expiry, prep trimmings) per meal → trend by menu item/day → recommendations (reduce portion, replace unpopular items) → dietician/menu committee; wet waste hand-over to composting/vendor (NC-016 log); waste per patient-day KPI.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- Active diet source of truth = OP-011/IP-003 orders; kitchen cannot change diet type (only preferences/late-tray operational fields); NPO active → no tray & ticket printed as "NPO — no meal" for ward awareness; discharge/transfer → list updates within 1 min.
- Allergen guard: recipe allergens ∩ patient allergens → block ticket unless dietician override; texture mismatch (IDDSI) → block.
- Cut-off enforcement; late trays need approval; counts frozen at cut-off for production (changes tracked).
- HACCP: cooking core temp ≥ 75 °C, hot-holding ≥ 63 °C at dispatch, cold ≤ 5 °C; out-of-range → hold/reheat/discard with log; fridge out-of-range > 30 min → alert; food sample retention per meal; handlers without valid medical fitness/FoSTaC cannot be rostered (NC-030 warning).
- POS: subsidy only for identified staff within limits; salary deduction requires consent & monthly cap; voids need supervisor; GST invoice details (FSSAI no., GSTIN); collections reconcile to NC-001 shift; tokens single-use with expiry.
- Attendant meals charged: to IP bill only with patient/attendant consent flag; else POS.
- Inventory: perishables FEFO, receiving temperature checks; recipe consumption posting daily; variance > 10 % flagged.
- Waste: recorded per meal; per patient-day KPI; monthly menu review action.
- Numbering `MEAL_TKT` daily, `POS` per counter/shift; audit on subsidy rules, voids, overrides.

## 13. Non-functional

- Volumes: 2000 beds → ~1,600 IP trays × 5 slots ≈ 8k patient meals/day, canteen 5–10k transactions/day (peaks 30/min at lunch), 100 tickets/min printing bursts; meal list regeneration < 5 s per slot; POS sale p95 < 150 ms; KDS realtime.
- Offline: POS works offline (queue sales, cached items/subsidy rules; identity via cached staff list) and syncs; ward widget queues delivery/intake; tray-line scanner offline for 1 h.
- Printing: tray labels (ZPL/ESC-POS), receipts, tokens, production sheets; i18n; WCAG 2.2 AA; touchscreen POS UX.
- Security: RLS; minimal PHI on labels; POS PCI-scope via EN-010; audit on voids/subsidy; sensor ingest tokens.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a dietician changes bed W5-12 to renal soft diet at 09:30 (before 10:00 lunch cut-off), then the lunch meal list updates within 1 min, kitchen board shows the change badge, and the printed ticket reads "Renal, IDDSI 6 – Soft".
2. Given the same change at 10:20 (after cut-off), then it enters the late-tray queue; supervisor approval produces the tray and marks it `late_added`.
3. Given a patient with peanut allergy and lunch recipe containing peanuts, then ticket printing is blocked with an allergen alert; dietician override with reason is required and logged.
4. Given OT scheduled at 14:00 with NPO from 06:00, then breakfast/lunch trays for that bed are `held_npo` and the ward widget shows NPO; when NPO clears, dinner is planned.
5. Given cart for Ward 7 dispatched at 12:35 for 12:30 serve time with holding temperature 60 °C (< 63 °C), then a HACCP non-conformance is logged, kitchen alerted, and the dispatch is flagged late/temperature.
6. Given a nurse marks tray delivered with intake 50 % and 2-star feedback, then OP-011 receives `meal.delivered` and NC-032 gets a low-feedback signal aggregated (not a ticket unless comment/complaint flagged).
7. Given a staff nurse (grade B, subsidy ₹40/lunch, 1 lunch/day) buys lunch ₹90 via salary deduction, then POS shows net ₹50 deduction; a second lunch the same day gets no subsidy; monthly file to NC-010 totals correctly.
8. Given a visitor pays via UPI QR, then the EN-010 callback marks payment, receipt prints with GST & FSSAI number, and the sale rolls into the counter's NC-001 shift.
9. Given a POS void by a cashier without supervisor PIN, then void is rejected; with supervisor PIN it is audited.
10. Given production of 800 dal portions at recipe 40 g/portion, then theoretical consumption 32 kg posts to NC-006 kitchen store; actual issue 36 kg → variance 12.5 % flagged.
11. Given a walk-in fridge reads 9 °C for 35 minutes, then an alarm reaches kitchen manager & facility and a hygiene non-conformance draft is created.
12. Given the month's waste logs, then waste per patient-day and top-5 wasted items are shown with a menu review recommendation.
13. Given a POS terminal offline for 20 min, then sales queue locally with sequential numbers and sync without duplicates.
