## 1. Purpose

NC-025 keeps the building running safely: **maintenance requests & work orders** for all civil/electrical/plumbing/HVAC/carpentry/medical-gas issues (from staff, housekeeping, BME, patients via NC-032) with SLA, technician dispatch, materials and closure; **planned maintenance & statutory checks** for building systems (fire equipment, lifts, DG sets, UPS, transformers, HVAC/AHUs, chillers, water plants, STP/ETP, medical gas manifold/plant, boilers) via NC-002 PM engine; **utilities & energy monitoring** (electricity, water, diesel, medical gases, LPG/PNG) with meter readings/IoT, cost allocation and conservation KPIs; **critical-area environmental monitoring** (OT/ICU/pharmacy/lab temperature, humidity, pressure, air changes; RO water and drinking water quality; medical gas alarms) with alerts and NABH evidence; **room & space booking** (conference/training rooms, auditoriums, guest rooms); AMC tracking view; outsourced FM contractor management; and facility rounds/inspections. It complements NC-002 (asset/AMC/PM engine) and NC-020 (medical devices).

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Any staff** (web/NC-014 mobile; QR on space/asset): raise maintenance request with photo/location/priority; track; rate.
- **Facility manager** (desktop): triage requests, assign technicians/vendors, monitor SLA, PM calendar & statutory checks, utilities dashboard, environmental alarms, contractor SLA, budgets.
- **Technician** (phone): my work orders, accept/start/complete with checklist, materials used (NC-006 issue), photos, permits-to-work (hot work, electrical isolation, confined space), sign-off.
- **BMS operator/electrician on shift**: live BMS/IoT alarms (power failure, DG start, UPS battery, chiller trip, AHU filter, medical gas low pressure, water tank low), log actions; meter readings rounds.
- **OT/ICU in-charge, pharmacy, lab, dialysis**: view environmental parameters, acknowledge out-of-range, request corrective action; dialysis RO water test entries.
- **Admin/Quality/Finance**: NABH FMS evidence (rounds, drills, checks), utility cost per bed/department, energy savings.
- **Vendors**: AMC visits/breakdowns via NC-021 portal.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Maintenance request → work order

1. **Requester** scans space/asset QR or picks location → category enum(electrical/plumbing/hvac/carpentry/civil/paint/medical_gas/lift/fire_system/it_cabling(→NC-028)/pest_control/signage/furniture/other), description, photo, priority (self-assessed; facility can adjust), impact (patient area/critical service down) → **System** creates work order (shared `asset_work_orders` NC-002, kind=corrective, module=facility), SLA by category/priority (e.g. critical: OT/ICU power/AC/gas 30 min response; high 2 h; normal 8 h; low 48 h) → auto-route to trade team/technician on shift (NC-030) or AMC vendor (NC-002 contract) → notifications → Event `facility.workorder.created`.
2. **Technician** accepts → travels → start (QR scan) → **permit-to-work** if required (hot work/electrical isolation/height/confined space: checklist, approver, LOTO) → diagnosis → materials (spares issue NC-006; purchase request NC-005 if unavailable → status `parts_pending`) → fix → checklist/photos → completion → requester acceptance (auto after 24 h) → close → SLA metrics; re-open within 7 days links to same WO → Event `facility.workorder.completed|closed`.
3. Exceptions: duplicate detection (same space/category open WO → merge), escalations on SLA breach (EN-038), critical area impact → notify BME/nursing/OT and NC-019 (fire/gas), vendor SLA tracking (NC-002/NC-021), cost capture (labour, materials, vendor) → NC-008 cost centre of location; project-type works (renovation) grouped into a project with milestones.

### 3.2 Planned maintenance & statutory checks (via NC-002 PM engine)

- PM plans for facility assets & systems: fire extinguishers (monthly visual, annual refill/HPT per IS 2190), fire hydrant/sprinkler/pump weekly test, fire alarm panel & smoke detectors, emergency lights, lifts (monthly service, annual inspection & licence NC-023), DG sets (weekly no-load run, load test, emissions/PCB), UPS/batteries, transformers/HT panels (annual shutdown maintenance, oil tests, earthing resistance), AHUs (filter change, coil cleaning; HEPA integrity/DOP annually for OT), chillers/cooling towers (legionella control), water tanks cleaning (6-monthly) & potability tests (IS 10500), RO plant (dialysis: daily chlorine/hardness, monthly microbiology/endotoxin per ISO 23500), STP/ETP parameters & consent, medical gas manifold/LMO/vacuum/compressor (daily rounds, alarms test, quarterly purity/pressure tests IS 7396), boilers, elevators, kitchen exhaust, pest control (schedule & log), building structural/roof/façade audits, signage & wayfinding audit → work orders with checklists → compliance dashboards → statutory renewals & inspections mirrored to NC-023.

### 3.3 Utilities & energy monitoring (`facility.energy`)

1. Meters master (electricity main/sub-meters by department/floor, DG, solar, water (municipal/borewell/RO), diesel, medical gas cylinders/LMO level, LPG/PNG) → readings via IoT/BMS (EN-042 Modbus/BACnet/MQTT) or manual rounds (phone form with meter QR) → consumption, cost (tariff slabs, demand charges, power factor), per-bed/per-patient-day KPIs, department allocation (NC-008), anomaly alerts (leak: night water flow; energy spike), DG run hours & diesel per kWh, solar generation, PF penalty avoidance; **energy budget** (NC-022) & conservation projects tracking (LED, VFD, chiller optimisation) with savings verification → Event `facility.utility.reading.recorded|anomaly`.
2. Utility bills (NC-009 AP) vs metered consumption reconciliation.

### 3.4 Critical-area environmental monitoring (`facility.bms`, `facility.medical_gas`, `facility.water_quality`)

- OT/ICU/NICU/isolation rooms/pharmacy cold rooms/lab/blood bank: temperature, RH, differential pressure, ACH (from AHU), HEPA status → sensors (EN-042) or manual 4-hourly logs → out-of-range → alarm to BMS operator + area in-charge + ICN (pressure loss in isolation) → acknowledgement + corrective WO; OT validation reports (annual/after HEPA change) stored; medical gas: pipeline pressures/alarms per zone, cylinder/LMO stock & consumption, manifold changeovers, low-pressure alarms → nursing/anaesthesia notified (IP-006/IP-009), backup cylinders dispatch; water: RO for dialysis daily tests & monthly micro (OP-012/IP-022 evidence), potable water tests, cooling tower legionella; DG/UPS status (power failure events, changeover time), fire panel alarms (→ NC-019 code Red flow) → all logged for NABH FMS.

### 3.5 Room & space booking (`facility.room_booking`)

- Bookable spaces (conference rooms, training halls, auditorium, seminar rooms, guest rooms/doctors' quarters, VIP lounges) with capacity, amenities (AV, VC, catering NC-033), rules (who can book, lead time, approvals for auditorium), calendar (day/week), booking request → conflicts checked → approval → setup tasks (housekeeping NC-018, IT NC-028 for AV, canteen NC-033 for refreshments) → check-in/no-show release; recurring bookings; utilisation reports; integrates NC-027 training calendar & OP-001 doctor schedules (not clinical rooms — those are in EN-027/OP-001).

### 3.6 Facility rounds, inspections & drills

- Daily engineering rounds (checklists: plant rooms, DG, panels, gas manifold, water tanks, roofs), weekly safety rounds (NABH FMS.2: hazards, fire exits, signage, electrical hazards, wet floors), monthly facility inspection with Quality → findings → work orders/CAPA (NC-015); fire drills coordinated with NC-019 (records shared); mock power-failure drill; hazardous materials inventory (MSDS NC-004; diesel, LPG, chemicals) & spill kits.

### 3.7 Outsourced FM contractor (`facility.contractor`)

- Contract (NC-031/NC-021): manpower per trade/shift, SLA response/resolution, PM completion %, penalties; contractor attendance (NC-029), performance monthly → invoice verification (NC-005/NC-009).

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- SLA matrix by category × priority × area criticality (config; defaults above); critical-area WOs auto-notify area in-charge & BME; escalation at 80 %/100 %/+50 % via EN-038.
- WO cannot close without checklist/photos where template requires and requester acceptance (auto-accept 24 h); reopen within 7 days keeps SLA history; materials must reference NC-006 issue or vendor bill.
- Permit-to-work mandatory for hot work/electrical HT/height/confined space; WO cannot start until permit approved; LOTO points recorded and released at close.
- Env monitoring: out-of-range readings require acknowledgement within 15 min (critical areas) and corrective WO if persists > 30 min; OT out-of-range during a case → notify OT in-charge/anaesthetist (IP-006) — decision to continue is clinical, logged.
- Medical gas low-pressure alarm → immediate notification to nursing/anaesthesia of affected zone + facility on-call; backup cylinder dispatch task; changeover logged.
- Water tests: RO daily test missing by 10:00 → dialysis in-charge alert; failed micro/endotoxin → dialysis notified (clinical decision), disinfection WO; potable water failure → advisory & retest.
- Meter readings: monotonic; rollover handled; manual reading with photo; anomaly thresholds per meter; utility bill variance > 5 % vs metered → review.
- Room booking: no overlap (DB exclusion constraint); approval for restricted spaces; no-show auto-release after 15 min (config); guest rooms may be chargeable (OP-005 misc/NC-009).
- Rounds findings with severity high → WO within SLA; drills scheduled per NABH with NC-019.
- Numbering `FM_REQ`, `WO` (shared), `BOOK`; retention: WOs 5 years, env logs 2 years detail, statutory checks life of asset.

## 7. Domain Events (outbox)

- `facility.request.created|triaged|merged`, `facility.workorder.created|assigned|started|parts_pending|completed|closed|reopened|sla_breached` {wo_id, space, category, priority, minutes, cost} → requester notifications, NC-002 (asset history), NC-018 (unblock spaces), NC-020 (utility-related equipment), NC-021 (vendor SLA), NC-008 (cost), NC-011.
- `facility.permit.approved|closed`, `facility.alarm.raised|acknowledged|cleared` {system, point, severity} → NC-019 (fire → code Red), IP-006/IP-009 (gas/AC/power), BME, on-call.
- `facility.env.out_of_range|acknowledged` {space, param, value} → area in-charge, ICN (isolation/OT), IP-006 board.
- `facility.utility.reading.recorded|anomaly` → NC-008 allocation, NC-022 budget, dashboards.
- `facility.water.test.recorded|failed` → OP-012/IP-022 (dialysis), IP-012 (ICN), EN-003.
- `facility.gas.low|changeover` → nursing/anaesthesia zone, NC-006 cylinder reorder.
- `facility.booking.requested|approved|cancelled|checked_in` → NC-018 setup task, NC-028 AV ticket, NC-033 refreshments order, NC-027 calendar.
- `facility.round.finding` → WO/CAPA (NC-015).
- Consumes: `housekeeping.task.blocked (maintenance)`, `bme.breakdown.logged (utility cause)`, `security.code.activated (Red)`, `iot.bms.alarm|meter.reading|sensor.reading` (EN-042), `asset.pm.due|completed` (NC-002 facility assets), `licence.expiring|expired` (NC-023 lift/fire/DG), `grievance.ticket.created (facility category)` (NC-032), `training.session.scheduled` (NC-027 room need), `roster.published` (NC-030 technicians), `purchase.grn.accepted` (spares → parts_pending WOs resume).

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given a nurse scans ICU-3 AHU QR and reports "AC not cooling" with impact patient_critical, then a WO with 30-min response SLA is created, HVAC technician on shift and BME are notified, and the ICU in-charge sees status.
2. Given a WO requiring hot work, then start is blocked until a hot-work permit is approved by the facility manager; LOTO points are recorded and released on close.
3. Given OT-2 temperature reads 25.5 °C (limit 24 °C) from sensor for 20 min, then an out-of-range alarm is shown on the OT board and facility board; unacknowledged after 15 min escalates; after 30 min a corrective WO is auto-created.
4. Given medical oxygen zone pressure alarm on Ward 8, then nursing station of Ward 8, anaesthesia on-call and facility on-call receive alerts within 5 s and a backup cylinder dispatch task is created.
5. Given a monthly electricity bill of 520,000 units vs metered 480,000 (variance 8 % > 5 %), then the bill is flagged for review before AP approval.
6. Given a night water flow of 4 kL between 01:00–04:00 on a meter with baseline 0.5 kL, then a leak anomaly is raised.
7. Given RO water daily chlorine test not entered by 10:00, then dialysis in-charge and facility get an alert; a failed endotoxin result triggers dialysis notification and a disinfection WO.
8. Given two bookings overlapping for Auditorium 14:00–16:00 and 15:00–17:00, then the second is rejected by the DB constraint with a conflict message.
9. Given a room booking approved with refreshments and AV, then a NC-033 refreshments order and NC-028 AV ticket are created and linked.
10. Given a fire alarm from the panel, then facility alarm is raised and NC-019 receives an event to start Code Red flow.
11. Given a WO closed but requester reopens within 7 days, then the same WO reopens, SLA history preserved, and technician performance reflects the reopen.
12. Given fire-extinguisher monthly checks (200 units) generated, then a compliance % appears on the statutory dashboard and overdue extinguishers list is exportable for NABH.
