## 1. Purpose

OP-037 provides system-aware AYUSH consoles: **Ayurveda** (Prakriti/Vikriti, Dosha-Dushya, Ashtavidha/Dashavidha pareeksha, Nidana Panchaka, Roga/Rogi bala, Samprapti; classical & proprietary medicine Rx with Anupana/Kala; Pathya-Apathya; **Panchakarma** planning — Purvakarma/Pradhana/Paschat karma with day-wise therapy scheduling & therapy logs), **Homoeopathy** (case-taking, repertorisation notes, remedy/potency/dose/repetition, follow-up with Hering's law/Kent's observations, placebo policy), **Unani** (Mizaj, Akhlat, Nabz, Ilaj bit-Tadbeer regimens: Hijama/Fasd/Dalak/Hammam), **Siddha** (Envagai thervu, Mukkutram, Naadi, Varmam), **Yoga & Naturopathy** (assessment, asana/pranayama prescriptions, hydro/mud/massage therapy schedules, diet/fasting), plus therapy-room scheduling, therapist gender-matching, consumables (oils/medicines) consumption, integrative referrals, packages/wellness billing, NAMASTE coding and ABDM AYUSH EHR export. Framework per OP-025 §0; scheduling reuses OP-015 therapy engine.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **AYUSH physician** (desktop/tablet; 30–60 pts/day): system-specific case-taking, diagnosis (NAMASTE + ICD-11 TM2, ICD-10 for co-morbidity), Rx from system formulary (classical text refs), therapy/Panchakarma course plan, diet/lifestyle (Dinacharya/Ritucharya), follow-up.
- **Panchakarma/therapy staff**: daily therapy worklist per room/therapist, pre-therapy checks (vitals, food, bowel), perform & log (oil qty, duration, temperature for Swedana, observations), post-therapy instructions, consumables scan.
- **AYUSH pharmacist**: dispense classical/proprietary/homoeopathic remedies (potency/scale), in-house preparations (Kwatha/Churna) batches, Schedule E1 register.
- **Reception/billing**: course/package booking, room & therapist slots (gender-matched), advances.
- **Patient**: therapy schedule, diet/lifestyle sheets, yoga videos (OP-038), reminders.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Consultation (system-specific)

1. Console tabs by system: Case-taking · Diagnosis · Rx · Therapy/Panchakarma · Diet & Lifestyle · Yoga plan · Follow-up.
2. **Ayurveda**: Prakriti assessment (questionnaire → Vata/Pitta/Kapha % ; stored once, re-assessable), Vikriti, Dosha/Dushya/Srotas involvement, Ashtavidha pareeksha (Nadi, Mutra, Mala, Jihva, Shabda, Sparsha, Drik, Akriti), Dashavidha (Prakriti, Vikriti, Sara, Samhanana, Pramana, Satmya, Satva, Ahara-shakti, Vyayama-shakti, Vaya), Agni/Koshtha, Nidana Panchaka; **diagnosis** NAMASTE Ayurveda code (e.g. Amavata) + ICD-11 TM2 + optional ICD-10 (M06.9), Roga bala/Rogi bala; **Rx**: medicine (classical: Yoga name + reference text/edition e.g. "Dashamoola Kwatha — Sahasrayogam"; proprietary: brand/manufacturer/batch), form (Churna/Vati/Kwatha/Asava-Arishta/Ghrita/Taila/Bhasma/Rasa), dose & unit (g/ml/tablets/Ratti), Anupana (honey/warm water/milk), Kala (before/after food, Pratah, Sayam…), duration; Schedule E1 flag; **Pathya-Apathya** (diet do/don't), Dinacharya/Ritucharya advice, Yoga plan.
3. **Homoeopathy**: detailed case (mental/generals/particulars, modalities, thermal, cravings, dreams), miasm, totality, repertorisation summary (external software export attach), remedy (name, potency scale C/X/LM/Q, dose, repetition, vehicle globules/liquid), follow-up assessment (better/same/worse, Kent's observations, aggravation), placebo/Sac lac policy (config; documented as such internally).
4. **Unani**: Mizaj (temperament), Akhlat, Nabz (pulse) characters, Baul/Baraz, Asbab-e-Sitta Zarooriya, diagnosis (NAMASTE Unani), Ilaj: Ilaj bil-Ghiza (diet), Ilaj bil-Dawa (single/compound; Murakkab), Ilaj bit-Tadbeer regimens (Hijama, Fasd, Irsal-e-Alaq/leech, Dalak, Hammam, Riyazat).
5. **Siddha**: Envagai thervu (8-fold exam: Naadi, Sparisam, Naa, Niram, Mozhi, Vizhi, Malam, Moothiram), Mukkutram, Udal thathukkal, diagnosis (NAMASTE Siddha), Rx (Chooranam/Kudineer/Parpam/Chendhuram…), Varmam therapy, Thokkanam.
6. **Yoga & Naturopathy**: lifestyle/diet assessment, fitness screen, asana/pranayama/meditation prescription (library with contraindications e.g. inversions in HTN/glaucoma; pregnancy modifications), naturopathy treatments (hydrotherapy, mud pack, massage, fasting/diet therapy schedules), goals & outcomes (weight, BP, glucose, sleep, stress scales).
7. Sign → visit summary (system-specific print, regional language) → `ayush.consult.signed`.

### 3.2 Panchakarma / therapy course planning & scheduling

1. Physician plans **course**: e.g. Purvakarma (Snehapana with dose schedule/Deepana-Pachana; Abhyanga + Swedana days), Pradhana karma (Vamana/Virechana/Basti (Niruha/Anuvasana — Kala/Karma/Yoga basti sequences)/Nasya/Raktamokshana), Paschat karma (Samsarjana krama diet steps) → day-wise **therapy schedule** (procedure, medicine/oil & quantity, duration, room type e.g. Droni table/steam chamber/Shirodhara stand, therapist gender requirement, pre-conditions) → consent (EN-028) → package/estimate (OP-023/RC-008), IP admission if `ayush.panchakarma_ip` → booking via OP-015 engine (rooms/therapists/equipment resources) → `ayush.course.planned`.
2. Unani regimens/Siddha Varmam/naturopathy schedules use the same course-scheduler.

### 3.3 Therapy execution & log

- Therapist worklist (room/day) → check-in → **pre-therapy checklist** (vitals BP/pulse from OP-007, last meal time, bowel/bladder, menstruation status for female therapies, contraindication reconfirmation) → perform → **therapy log**: start/end, medicine/oil name & batch & quantity used (deduct NC-006/OP-003), temperature/duration (Swedana), Shirodhara flow, Basti volume & retention time, Vamana/Virechana Vega count & Lakshanas (Samyak/Ayoga/Atiyoga), adverse events, patient tolerance, post-therapy advice → physician daily review & modifications → consumables billing per policy → `ayush.therapy.logged`.

### 3.4 Pharmacy & dispensing (via OP-003 with AYUSH masters)

- Masters: classical formulations (with text references, ingredients), proprietary ASU/H products (manufacturer, licence no., batch/expiry), homoeopathic remedies (potency matrix), in-house preparation batches (Kwatha/Churna preparation log with raw drug consumption); Schedule E1 register (poisonous drugs — Rx by registered practitioner, quantity limits); dispensing labels (Anupana/Kala instructions in regional language); stock, expiry (Asava-Arishta long shelf life vs Kwatha short) via FEFO.

### 3.5 Integrative care & referrals

- Allopathic ⇄ AYUSH referrals (OP-021) with shared problem list; drug–herb interaction alerts (EN-029 seed list e.g. warfarin + Guggulu/Ashwagandha, antidiabetics + Karela/Gymnema; heavy-metal Bhasma caution with renal/hepatic disease; pregnancy contraindications), duplicate steroid-like use flags; lab monitoring suggestions (LFT/RFT for Rasa aushadhi long-term).

### 3.6 Exceptions

- Contraindication found at pre-therapy check → therapy skipped/rescheduled with physician approval; adverse event → NC-015; female patient requiring female therapist unavailable → reschedule; package early exit → pro-rata billing rule; offline: therapy log cached.

## 4. Data Model (schema `specialty`)

- **ayush_consults**: id, hospital_id, branch_id, patient_id, encounter_id, system enum(ayurveda/homoeopathy/unani/siddha/yoga_naturopathy), assessment jsonb (system-specific typed sections: prakriti{v,p,k}, vikriti, ashtavidha, dashavidha, agni, koshtha | homoeo{mentals, generals, particulars, miasm, totality} | unani{mizaj, akhlat, nabz} | siddha{envagai, mukkutram} | yoga{lifestyle, fitness}), diagnoses jsonb ([{namaste_code, namaste_term, icd11_tm2, icd10?, primary}]), plan jsonb, pathya_apathya jsonb, lifestyle_advice jsonb, form_response_id?, signed_by/at, version; index (hospital_id, patient_id, at desc), (system).
- **ayush_prakriti** (once per patient, re-assessable): patient_id, vata_pct, pitta_pct, kapha_pct, dominant, questionnaire_response_id, assessed_at, by.
- **ayush_prescriptions** (extends OP-002 rx with AYUSH fields): rx_line_id, system, medicine_id (mdm ayush), classical_ref jsonb (text, chapter), form, dose numeric, unit, anupana, kala, potency (homoeo), scale, repetition, duration_days, schedule_e1 bool, notes.
- **ayush_medicine_master** (mdm/EN-027): id, hospital_id?, system, name, type enum(classical/proprietary/homoeo_remedy/raw_drug/inhouse), ingredients jsonb, reference_text, form, manufacturer, licence_no, schedule_e1 bool, contraindications jsonb, interactions jsonb, hsn, gst_pct, item_id (NC-006 link).
- **ayush_courses**: id, patient_id, consult_id, type enum(panchakarma/unani_regimen/siddha_therapy/naturopathy/yoga_programme), phases jsonb ([{phase (purva/pradhana/paschat), procedures[]}]), plan_days jsonb ([{day, procedure_code, medicine_id, qty, duration_min, room_type, therapist_gender_req, preconditions}]), consent_id, package_booking_id?, admission_id?, status enum(planned/consented/scheduled/in_progress/completed/aborted), start_date, end_date, signed_by/at.
- **ayush_therapy_sessions** (OP-015 session shell + extension): session_id, course_id, day_no, procedure_code, room_id, therapist_ids uuid[], prechecks jsonb, medicines_used jsonb ([{medicine_id, batch_id, qty, uom}]), params jsonb (temp, duration, flow, volume, retention_min, vega_count, lakshana enum(samyak/ayoga/atiyoga)), adverse jsonb, tolerance, post_advice, physician_review jsonb, status enum(scheduled/done/skipped/rescheduled), at; index (course_id, day_no).
- **ayush_procedure_master** (mdm): code, system, name, category, default_duration, room_type, requires_consent, therapist_count, gender_match_required bool, consumables_kit jsonb, contraindications jsonb, tariff_code.
- **ayush_therapy_rooms**: id, branch_id, name, type enum(abhyanga/swedana_chamber/shirodhara/basti/vamana/nasya/hydro/mud/massage/yoga_hall/hijama), equipment, gender_zone enum(male/female/any), active.
- **yoga_prescriptions**: consult_id, items jsonb ([{asana/pranayama code, duration, reps, modifications, video_ref}]), contraindication_check jsonb; **naturopathy_schedules** analogous to courses.
- **ayush_followups**: consult_id, at, response enum(better/same/worse/aggravation), observations jsonb (Kent's/lakshana), rx_change, next_at.
- **schedule_e1_register**: hospital_id, medicine_id, rx_id, patient_id, qty, dispensed_by, at (also mirrored in OP-003 registers).
- Enums: `ayush_system`, `medicine_type`, `course_status`, `therapy_status`, `lakshana`.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- Practitioner–system binding: a doctor may prescribe only medicines of systems in their registration (NCISM/NCH); cross-system Rx blocked (config per state rules); allopathic drugs not prescribable from AYUSH console (read-only view of allopathic meds).
- Diagnosis: NAMASTE code mandatory (system-specific), ICD-11 TM2 auto-mapped where mapping exists; ICD-10 co-morbidity optional; ABDM AYUSH EHR export uses these.
- Schedule E1 medicines: only registered practitioner, quantity/duration limits, register entry on dispensing; Rasa/Bhasma long-term (> 45 days config) → LFT/RFT monitoring prompt; pregnancy/lactation contraindication checks; drug–herb interaction alerts when patient on allopathic meds (shared med list).
- Panchakarma safety: Vamana/Virechana require Snehapana completion & Samyak snigdha lakshana recorded; contraindication checks (pregnancy, severe cardiac, uncontrolled HTN, menstruation for certain therapies, age extremes); Basti volumes within age/weight guidance; therapist gender match enforced for gender-sensitive procedures unless patient consents otherwise; consent before Pradhana karma & invasive (Raktamokshana/leech, Kshara sutra, Hijama/Fasd); vitals pre-therapy mandatory; adverse events → physician review before next session.
- Homoeopathy: potency/scale required; placebo dispensing internal flag only (patient print shows medicine as per hospital policy — legal review); repetition rules.
- Yoga: contraindication engine (asana × condition) warns; pregnancy modifications.
- Courses: day plans generate OP-015 bookings; skip requires physician approval; package sessions decrement; early termination pro-rata rule; medicine/oil consumption deducts stock at log; billing per session/package/consumables policy.
- Records immutable after sign; Prakriti stored longitudinally; retention clinical.

## 13. Non-functional

- Volumes: 300 AYUSH visits/day enterprise, 200 therapy sessions/day (30 rooms), courses 40 concurrent IP; NAMASTE search < 150 ms (FTS/trigram); therapy log save p95 < 250 ms; offline therapist tablet; print: system-specific Rx & diet sheets in regional scripts (Devanagari, Tamil, Urdu RTL, Malayalam), therapy schedule card; accessibility & i18n; GST classification per item.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria (plus OP-025 §0.9)

1. Given an Ayurveda consult, when diagnosis "Amavata" is picked from NAMASTE, then ICD-11 TM2 code auto-maps and optional ICD-10 M06.9 can be added; the ABDM AYUSH bundle export includes all three.
2. Given a homoeopath prescribes an Ayurvedic classical formulation, then Rx is blocked (cross-system rule) unless the practitioner has that system registered.
3. Given a Panchakarma course with Virechana on day 8, when Snehapana Samyak lakshana is not recorded by day 7, then the day-8 session is flagged "not cleared" and requires physician clearance.
4. Given a female patient's Abhyanga scheduled and only male therapists are available in the slot, then booking fails gender-match and offers alternatives; override requires patient consent record.
5. Given a therapist logs 150 mL Dhanwantharam taila (batch X) for a session, then stock deducts by batch and the consumable line posts per package/billing policy.
6. Given a patient on warfarin (allopathic list) is prescribed a Guggulu formulation, then EN-029 shows an interaction alert requiring acknowledgement.
7. Given a Schedule E1 medicine dispensed, then the register records practitioner, patient, quantity, and quantity limits are enforced.
8. Given a Vamana session logs Vega count and "Atiyoga" lakshana, then an adverse flag reaches the physician and next-day sessions are held until reviewed.
9. Given a yoga prescription includes Sirsasana for a hypertensive patient, then a contraindication warning appears and requires modification/override.
10. Given a course aborted on day 4 of 14 in a package, then pro-rata billing/credit note per policy is generated.
11. Given a therapist attempts to sign a consult, then 403 and audit.
12. Given a homoeopathic follow-up records "aggravation", then the follow-up interval and remedy hold prompt appear per protocol.
