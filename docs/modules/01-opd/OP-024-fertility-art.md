## 1. Purpose
OP-024 runs a fertility centre end-to-end: couple registration with both partners linked and ART Act eligibility checks, infertility work-up (hormones, semen analysis, tubal/uterine evaluation), cycle tracking (menstrual log, ovulation prediction, hormone monitoring, folliculometry growth charts), protocol-driven IUI and IVF/ICSI cycles (antagonist/long agonist/natural/mini-IVF/FET protocols → auto-generated medication & monitoring calendars with injection reminders), an embryology lab module (OPU/oocyte grading, sperm preparation, insemination/ICSI, fertilisation checks, embryo grading Day 3 Gardner Day 5, time-lapse/photo storage, PGT biopsy, transfer, vitrification), cryopreservation inventory (tanks/canisters/goblets/straws with double witnessing, storage consents & renewals), donor/bank management with anonymised codes, outcome tracking (β-hCG → clinical pregnancy → ongoing → live birth), mandatory National ART Registry reporting, and analytics of success rates per protocol/doctor/age band.

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done
- **Fertility specialist** (desktop; 20–40 consults + 5–15 scans/day): assess couple, plan protocol, prescribe stimulation, review monitoring (E2/follicles), decide trigger/OPU/transfer, counsel, sign consents, follow outcomes.
- **ART nurse/coordinator**: cycle calendar, injection teaching & reminders, scheduling scans/labs, consents, day-of coordination (OPU/ET), pregnancy tests, registry data.
- **Embryologist** (lab desktop/tablet in clean room, gloves — large controls; RFID/barcode witnessing): receive oocytes, sperm prep, ICSI/IVF, culture checks, grading with images, biopsy, ET prep, vitrification/thaw, cryo inventory, QC (incubator logs, KPIs).
- **Andrologist**: semen analysis (WHO 2021), sperm prep, TESA/PESA specimens, sperm freezing.
- **Counsellor**: mandatory counselling sessions (ART Act) with notes.
- **Anaesthetist**: OPU sedation (IP-024).
- **Billing/coordinator**: cycle packages (OP-023), payments before steps, insurance (rare).
- **Compliance officer**: registrations, registry submissions, consent audits, donor records, inspections.

## 3. Core Workflows
### 3.1 Couple registration & eligibility
1. Register **couple profile**: female partner + male partner (both UHIDs linked `couple_id`; single woman allowed per Act) → identity/age verification (Aadhaar/ID; ART Act age limits enforced: woman 21–50, man 21–55 → hard block with legal override reason logged), marital status/declaration per Act, address; **infertility history**: duration, type (primary/secondary), previous treatments/cycles (external history import), obstetric history, medical/surgical, lifestyle; baseline investigations: female (AMH, FSH/LH/E2 day 2–3, TSH/PRL, HbA1c, infectious screen HIV/HBsAg/HCV/VDRL, rubella IgG, TVS AFC, HSG/SIS/hysteroscopy, karyotype if indicated) and male (semen analysis WHO 2021 params: volume, concentration, total motility, progressive, morphology Kruger, vitality, DFI optional; hormones; infectious screen) → auto-pulled from OP-004; **mandatory counselling** (session recorded, counsellor sign) → treatment plan.
2. Consents (EN-028 ART Act templates): treatment consent (IUI/IVF/ICSI), anaesthesia, cryopreservation & storage duration/fate (use/donate for research/discard), donor gamete/embryo (recipient & donor forms via bank), PGT, embryo transfer number policy, complications (OHSS) info; e-sign both partners + witness; versioned.
### 3.2 Cycle tracking (pre-treatment / natural / OI-TI)
- Menstrual calendar (LMP, cycle length, flow), ovulation prediction (calendar/OPK/USG), hormone monitoring (LH/E2/P4 with graphs), **folliculometry**: per scan record follicles per ovary (sizes mm list, dominant follicle, endometrium thickness/pattern) → **growth chart** (follicle size vs cycle day, expected 1–2 mm/day), triggers timed-intercourse/IUI advice; PC-PNDT Form F auto-generated for each TVS on OP-008 (mandatory fields, signatory).
### 3.3 IUI cycle
1. Protocol: natural / OI (letrozole/clomiphene) / gonadotropin; monitoring scans → trigger (hCG) → IUI timing 24–36 h; **semen sample**: collection (partner or donor code from bank), abstinence days, identity witnessing (barcode/RFID chain of custody), **sperm preparation** (density gradient/swim-up: pre/post counts, motility, volume inseminated) → insemination procedure (OP-010 template: catheter, ease, comments), **luteal support** Rx, β-hCG scheduling day 14 → outcome.
### 3.4 IVF/ICSI cycle
1. **Protocol selection**: antagonist / long agonist / short (flare) / mini-IVF / natural / modified natural / FET (natural / HRT / stimulated) / freeze-all → **medication schedule auto-generated** (start day, drug, dose, route, timing; e.g. FSH 225 IU daily from D2, GnRH antagonist from D6 or follicle ≥ 14 mm, trigger criteria) → cycle calendar (scans D2, D6, D8, D10…, labs) → patient app/WhatsApp **injection reminders** with video, nurse teaching log; dose adjustments per monitoring (E2/follicle counts) with rationale.
2. Monitoring visits: folliculometry (follicles ≥ 10/14/17 mm counts), E2/LH/P4 trend → decision support: OHSS risk (E2 > 3500 pg/mL, > 20 follicles → agonist trigger/freeze-all suggestion), premature LH rise flags, cancellation criteria (poor response) → **trigger** (hCG/GnRH agonist/dual) with exact time → OPU scheduled 34–36 h later (OT/day-care booking OP-010/IP-006, anaesthesia PAC IP-024, fasting instructions).
3. **OPU**: procedure record (OP-010 template: anaesthesia, needle, follicles aspirated per ovary, complications, blood loss) → **oocytes to lab** with witnessing (patient wristband ↔ dish label barcode/RFID; double-witness by 2 embryologists or electronic witnessing `art.witnessing_rfid`) → embryology takes over (3.5); husband sample collected same day (or frozen/donor) with witnessing.
4. **Embryo transfer**: day 3/5 selection (grading), number transferred (policy ≤ 2–3 per ICMR/age; justification if > policy), catheter, ease, USG-guided, embryologist & clinician double witness of embryo ID; luteal support; **cryopreservation** of surplus (3.6); post-transfer instructions; β-hCG day 12–14 scheduling.
5. Cycle cancellation/conversion (poor response → IUI; freeze-all for OHSS) with reasons; OHSS management (grading, admission).
### 3.5 Embryology lab (`art.embryology_lab`)
1. **Oocyte assessment**: number retrieved, maturity (MII/MI/GV), quality notes; **sperm prep** for IVF/ICSI (source, method, post-prep parameters); **insemination**: conventional IVF vs ICSI (oocytes injected, degenerated) at time T; **fertilisation check** 16–18 h (2PN, 1PN, 3PN, unfertilised) → fertilisation rate; **cleavage/embryo grading**: Day 2/3 (cell number, fragmentation %, symmetry — Istanbul consensus), Day 5/6 blastocyst **Gardner** (expansion 1–6, ICM A–C, TE A–C); morphokinetics if time-lapse (EmbryoScope/Geri via EN-042 or manual annotation), embryo **photos/videos** stored (OP-022 viewer; per embryo ID); assisted hatching; **PGT** (`art.pgt`): biopsy day (5/6), cells, tubing witness, lab send-out (OP-004 external), results (euploid/aneuploid/mosaic) — **no sex disclosure** (PC-PNDT: reports masked for sex chromosomes; only medically indicated X-linked disease with authorisation); disposition per embryo: transfer / vitrify / discard (consented) / research (consented) / donate (bank).
2. **Lab QC**: incubator logs (temp/CO2/O2 per shift or IoT), LN2 levels, air quality (VOC), media lot & expiry tracking (NC-006; every dish links lot), equipment PM (NC-020), KPIs (Vienna consensus: fertilisation rate ≥ 65 % ICSI, cleavage, blastulation ≥ 40 %, implantation, cryo-survival ≥ 90 %), witnessing compliance, incident log (dish drop, mismatch near-miss → NC-015 & registry complication reporting).
### 3.6 Cryopreservation inventory (`art.cryo_inventory`)
- Specimen types: oocytes, embryos (per embryo ID, day, grade), sperm (partner/donor/testicular), ovarian/testicular tissue (if offered); **vitrification record** (device/straw type, ID, date, embryologist, witness); **location**: tank → canister → cane/goblet → position; label barcode/RFID; storage consent & duration (renewal reminders yearly; ART Act consent for fate on expiry/death/divorce/non-payment; disposal only per written consent & Act rules with two-person witness & record); **thaw/warming** record (survival, re-expansion) linked to FET cycle; transfers between clinics (ART bank documentation, chain of custody, consents); LN2 tank monitoring (EN-042 level/temp alarms 24×7 → escalation call chain), tank inventory audits (physical count vs system), storage billing (annual fee, OP-005/OP-023).
### 3.7 Donor & bank management (`art.donor_bank`)
- Only registered ART banks supply gametes: bank master (registration no.), donor records with **anonymised donor code** (identity held by bank; clinic stores code, phenotype, blood group, screening dates, limits: oocyte donor once in lifetime & max 7 oocytes; sperm donor per Act rules), recipient matching notes, consents (donor via bank, recipient), traceability (which cycles used which donor code), insurance for oocyte donors (Act), no payment beyond permitted expenses; identity release only by court order — access restricted role.
### 3.8 Outcome tracking
- β-hCG (OP-004) day 12–14 → positive → repeat 48 h → **clinical pregnancy** (TVS at 6–7 weeks: sac, FHR, number; ectopic/miscarriage tracking) → **ongoing pregnancy** 12 weeks → hand-off to OP-040 antenatal → **live birth** outcome capture (date, gestational age, birth weight, mode, multiples, neonatal outcome; IP-011 or external follow-up call), complications (OHSS grade, bleeding, infection, ectopic, multiple pregnancy) → registry; analytics: pregnancy/live-birth rate per cycle started/OPU/ET, per protocol, per doctor, per age band (< 35, 35–37, 38–40, > 40), fresh vs frozen, donor vs own, cumulative per couple; embryology KPIs.
### 3.9 Compliance & registry
- ART clinic/bank registration & PC-PNDT licences tracked (NC-023) with expiry alerts; **National ART Registry** reporting (cycle-level data set per Rules: patient demographics anonymised, treatment, outcomes, complications, donor usage) — export/API per format; complication reporting to appropriate authority; records retention ≥ 10 y (Act) — locked; consent completeness checks block steps (no OPU without consents; no ET without transfer consent; no cryo without storage consent; no donor use without bank docs).
### 3.10 Exceptions
- Eligibility failure (age/marital) → block with legal override & audit; witnessing mismatch → hard stop + incident; consent withdrawn (either partner) → cycle/cryo actions blocked (Act: both partners' consent for embryo use); OHSS admission; cycle cancellation; sample not produced (frozen backup); LN2 alarm; power failure; offline: lab tablet caches worklists; grading entries queue; witnessing requires online RFID reads (fallback double manual witness with reason).

## 5. Business Rules & Validations
- ART Act eligibility hard checks (ages, marital declaration, donor limits, bank registration validity, counselling before treatment, both partners' consents for embryo creation/use/storage/disposal); overrides only with legal-officer role & reason; all Act-relevant records locked (no delete) and retained ≥ 10 y.
- PC-PNDT: every TVS/pelvic USG generates Form F via OP-008 (mandatory fields, signatory), sex-related terms validator on all ART documents/PGT reports (sex chromosome results masked by default; unmasking only for authorised X-linked indication with documented approval); no "gender selection" service codes.
- Witnessing: every gamete/embryo handling step requires identity match (RFID/barcode) or two-person manual witness; mismatch → hard stop, incident, cycle hold; embryo codes unique & printed on all dishes/straws.
- Protocol engine: medication schedule generated from template with dose rules; changes versioned; trigger→OPU interval 34–36 h enforced (warning outside); OHSS risk rules (E2/follicle thresholds) prompt; ET number > policy requires justification; luteal support mandatory prompt.
- Lab: fertilisation check window 16–18 h; grading day-specific fields; disposition requires consent id; discard requires 2 witnesses; media lot expiry check on use; incubator out-of-range → alert; PGT results only from accredited labs.
- Cryo: storage location unique; consent validity tracked; renewal reminders 60/30 days; expiry actions only per consent + Act; disposal/donation/transfer-out require both partners' consent (or per Act on death/divorce as per consent form); LN2 alarm escalation 24×7; physical audit reconciliation monthly.
- Donor: identity never stored in clinic system beyond bank code; usage limits enforced; oocyte donor once-in-lifetime flag from bank; recipient-donor blood group/phenotype match notes.
- Outcome fields mandatory before cycle closure (or "lost to follow-up" after 3 attempts logged); registry submission monthly with validation; complications reported per Rules.
- Billing: cycle packages (OP-023) with steps gated by payment policy (no OPU without dues per policy, config), storage fees annual; donor expenses per Act.
- Access: ART data tagged highly sensitive; care-team-only; counselling notes restricted; break-glass audited; exports watermarked; no ART flags on generic patient banner beyond "ART programme" (config).

5. **Andrology bench**: semen analysis form (WHO 2021 refs), sperm prep record, freezing.
6. **Outcomes & registry** (desktop): β-hCG follow-ups list, pregnancy tracking, live-birth capture, registry submission builder with validation errors.
7. **Compliance dashboard**: licences, consent completeness, donor usage limits, witness compliance, incidents; **Success-rate analytics** (dark): per protocol/doctor/age band, KPIs vs benchmarks (Vienna consensus).

## 13. Non-functional
- Volumes: 150–300 IVF cycles/month/centre, 30 monitoring scans/day, 5k cryo specimens per centre; lab console p95 < 200 ms; witness verification < 500 ms; calendar generation < 300 ms; embryo image upload background.
- Availability: lab & cryo functions must work during network outages (local cache + manual double witness with later reconciliation); alarms independent (IoT unit dials/SMS) plus system escalation.
- Security: highest-sensitivity classification; encrypted donor fields (pgcrypto/app-level), separate RLS policies for `art_*`; no ART details in generic timelines beyond configured summary; audit every read/write; retention ≥ 10 y locked; exports watermarked & approved.
- Print: cycle calendar (patient language), consent forms (Act templates), dish/straw labels (barcode/RFID, cryo-resistant), embryo report, cycle summary, registry forms.
- Accessibility/i18n: consent & instructions multilingual; large touch controls in lab; RTL-ready.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria
1. Given a female partner aged 51, when registering a couple for IVF, then registration is blocked with ART Act age rule; a legal-officer override with reason is audited.
2. Given counselling not recorded, when a cycle is created, then it stays `planned` and OPU/IUI cannot be scheduled until counselling and both partners' consents are signed.
3. Given protocol "antagonist" started D1, then the calendar shows FSH from D2, antagonist from D6, scans D2/D6/D8/D10, and the patient app receives daily injection reminders with the correct dose after each adjustment.
4. Given monitoring shows 24 follicles ≥ 11 mm and E2 4200 pg/mL, then an OHSS risk banner recommends agonist trigger/freeze-all and the decision is recorded.
5. Given trigger at 21:00 on day X, then OPU scheduling suggests 07:00–09:00 on X+2 (34–36 h) and warns outside that window; fasting instructions are sent.
6. Given oocytes handed to the lab, when the dish barcode does not match the patient wristband scan, then a hard-stop mismatch appears, an incident is created and the cycle is placed on hold until resolved.
7. Given 10 MII oocytes injected (ICSI) with 7 × 2PN at 17 h, then fertilisation rate 70 % is computed and each zygote gets a unique embryo code.
8. Given a Day-5 embryo graded 4AA, when disposition = vitrify without a valid storage consent, then it is blocked; with consent, a straw ID, tank/canister/goblet position (unique) and double witness are recorded and the specimen appears in cryo inventory.
9. Given a PGT report with sex chromosome data, then the stored/displayed result masks sex information; unmasking requires the authorised X-linked indication workflow and is audited.
10. Given a storage consent expiring in 30 days, then both partners are notified and the compliance dashboard lists it; disposal requires both partners' consent and two witnesses.
11. Given LN2 level below threshold, then a tank alarm escalates by call/SMS/push to the embryologist on-call within 60 s and is logged with acknowledgement.
12. Given β-hCG positive and TVS at 7 weeks with FHR, then the outcome shows clinical pregnancy; at 12 weeks ongoing → hand-off to OP-040 with an ANC booking; live-birth capture completes the cycle and feeds success-rate analytics per protocol/age band.
13. Given the monthly registry export, then validation lists cycles missing mandatory outcome fields and blocks submission until fixed or marked lost-to-follow-up (≥ 3 contact attempts logged).
14. Given a donor code used for a second oocyte donation cycle when the bank flags once-in-lifetime, then use is blocked.
15. Given a nurse without `art.lab.record`, then embryo grading calls return 403 and are audited.
