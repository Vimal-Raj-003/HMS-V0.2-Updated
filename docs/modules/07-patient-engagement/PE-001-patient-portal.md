## 1. Purpose

PE-001 is the patient's own account with the hospital on the web: passwordless OTP or ABHA login, a dashboard of what is next, appointment booking and management with live queue position, a chronological record of every visit with prescriptions, lab and imaging reports and discharge summaries, bills with online payment and receipts, insurance and claim status, management of family members and dependants with proper consent, ABHA linking and health-locker sharing, first-class **DPDP self-service** (consent centre, access/correction/erasure requests with tracked SLAs, nomination, grievance), document upload, feedback, and a notification preference centre — all as an installable, offline-tolerant, multilingual, accessible PWA that shares one backend with the mobile app (OP-020).

## 2. Users & Jobs-to-be-done

- **Patient** (phone-first browser, often 3G, often a shared device, often not the person who books): book/reschedule, know when to come, get reports without standing in a queue, pay a bill, download a prescription, share records with another doctor, ask a question, complain when something is wrong.
- **Family member / caregiver** (the highest-value user in India — the son booking for a parent): manage several profiles from one login, receive their alerts, pay their bills, see their reports.
- **Front office / call centre**: link a walk-in patient to a portal account, resend a link, book on the patient's behalf, explain why a report is not yet visible.
- **Doctor**: decide what of the clinical note the patient may see, and read what the patient has uploaded or entered (PROMs, diaries, external reports).
- **Privacy Officer / DPO**: run the DSAR queue, evidence consent, close grievances within the statutory period — PE-001 is where most of that traffic originates.
- **Billing / insurance**: fewer counter visits because the patient can see the bill, the estimate, the claim status and pay online.

## 3. Core Workflows

### 3.1 Access, identity and account linking

1. **Login**: mobile number → OTP (EN-009, DLT template, 6 digits, 5 min, rate-limited, WhatsApp fallback) → session (access 15 min, refresh 30 days rotating, device-bound). **ABHA login** (`portal.abha`) via ABHA number/address with ABDM OTP (EN-011) as an alternative. Optional email as a secondary identifier. No passwords for patients by design.
2. **Account ↔ patient linking**: the mobile is matched against the MPI (OP-001). One mobile may map to several UHIDs (family sharing one number is the norm) → the user picks, or requests a merge (routed to MRD). Unmatched mobiles get a limited "guest" account that can book and pay but sees no records until identity is verified at the desk or by ABHA match — **records are never released on an unverified phone-number match alone**.
3. **First-run consent** (DPDP): a plain-language notice in the chosen language with itemised purposes — care and treatment records, service communications, appointment reminders, follow-up/recall, marketing (separate, off by default), research (separate, off by default), ABDM sharing — each independently togglable, each withdrawable from the same screen, all recorded in the EN-028 ledger with timestamp, version of the notice and the interface used.
4. **Minors**: a profile for a person under 18 can exist only under a verified parent/guardian account with **verifiable parental consent** captured (guardian identity verified at the desk or via ABHA/DigiLocker), no behavioural tracking, no marketing. At 18 the profile is prompted to convert to its own account and the guardian's access lapses unless re-consented.
5. **Session safety**: idle timeout, device list with "sign out everywhere", masked identifiers on shared screens, and a "hide sensitive records" toggle (see §3.11).

### 3.2 Dashboard

- Above the fold: next appointment (with join/reschedule/directions), live queue position if checked in, pending payments, new reports since last visit, active prescriptions, follow-up due, and any action the hospital needs from the patient (documents for insurance, consent pending, feedback request). Cards are role-honest: nothing is shown that the patient cannot act on. Profile switcher (family) is one tap away.

### 3.3 Appointments

1. **Book**: specialty → doctor (profile: photo, qualifications, languages, fee, next available, tele-availability, rating if EN-030 publishing is enabled) or "earliest available"; branch filter; in-person vs tele; slot picker on real availability (OP-001); patient/family member; reason; prep instructions; **pay now** (EN-010) or pay at hospital per policy → confirmation with appointment number, token, QR for kiosk check-in (EN-013/EN-034), map link, and calendar file.
2. **Manage**: reschedule and cancel within policy (cut-off hours, refund rules via EN-010), waitlist join with auto-offer when a slot frees, recurring series view (dialysis/physio/chemo), and **teleconsult join** (`portal.teleconsult`, OP-018) with a pre-call device check.
3. **Queue** (EN-006): after check-in — position, patients ahead, estimated wait, room/floor, and step routing (vitals → doctor → lab). Auto-refreshing; push at "3 ahead" and "you are next". If the doctor is delayed beyond the threshold, an apology with a reschedule option.

### 3.4 Health records timeline

- One reverse-chronological timeline across OPD, IP, ER, tele, day-care and health checks. Per visit: doctor, department, **diagnosis (if released)**, vitals, **prescription** (PDF + structured list with dosing, download/share, refill request to OP-003), **lab reports** (PDF plus structured values with reference ranges and trend charts for repeated analytes), **radiology reports** and images (viewer link via EN-008 share, optional DICOM download), **discharge summary** (IP-002), procedure/OT summaries where released, certificates (vaccination OP-013, fitness, medical certificate), invoices and receipts, consent copies, and **education materials** prescribed (PE-003/OP-038).
- **Release rules** (configurable per hospital, enforced server-side): reports become visible after validation (OP-004/OP-008) and after an optional hold window; **critical results are never first delivered by portal** — the clinical team contacts the patient and the portal shows a "your doctor will contact you" state; sensitive categories (see §3.11) follow their own rules; full clinical notes appear only with doctor authorisation or after a configured interval, while a patient-friendly visit summary is always available.
- **Search & filter** by date, doctor, type; download-all (DPDP portability) as a structured archive (FHIR bundle + PDFs).

### 3.5 Bills, estimates, payments

- Outstanding bills (OP-005/IP-005) with itemisation in plain language, GST notes, **estimates and quotations** (RC-008) with accept/pay-advance, statement of account and any active **payment plan** (RC-005) with the next instalment, deposit top-up for admitted patients (IP-005), payment via EN-010 (UPI intent/collect, cards, net-banking, wallets), receipts (PDF with QR verification), refund status, and an 80D-friendly annual payment summary. Every payment is idempotent and confirmed only on gateway webhook.

### 3.6 Insurance & claim status (`portal.claims`)

- Policies on file (EN-002), pre-auth status in plain language (RC-002: "submitted → query → approved ₹1,20,000 valid till 25-Aug"), **expected patient share with the reason breakdown** (co-pay, non-payables, room-class difference), claim status (RC-001), documents the hospital needs from the patient (upload directly), and the reimbursement pack download when applicable. Scheme beneficiaries (RC-007) see "cashless — you should not pay anything" with the helpline.

### 3.7 Family & dependants (`portal.family`)

1. **Add an adult**: invite by their mobile → they consent by OTP on their own device → the requester receives the permissions the member grants (view records / book / pay, each independent, each revocable at any time by the member). No adult's records are ever exposed on someone else's say-so.
2. **Add a dependant** (child under 18, or an adult who cannot consent — with legally valid guardianship evidence verified at the desk): relationship and identity captured, guardianship documented, parental-consent record for minors, and an audit entry for every access.
3. Profile switcher across members; per-member notification routing; member-initiated revocation with immediate effect; nomination (DPDP) so a nominee may exercise rights if the patient dies or becomes incapacitated.

### 3.8 ABHA & health-locker sharing (`portal.abha`)

- Create or link an **ABHA** (EN-011), view linked care contexts, **share records** with another provider through the ABDM consent flow (grant, view active consents with purpose and expiry, revoke), **fetch** records from other facilities (HIU) with consent, and view the consent artefact log. Scan-and-share QR at the hospital's front desk is mirrored here. ABDM consent is kept distinct from hospital DPDP consent in the UI so patients are not confused between the two regimes.

### 3.9 DPDP consent centre & DSAR self-service (`portal.dsar`)

1. **Consent centre**: every purpose with its current state, when and how it was given, the notice version, and a one-tap withdraw. Withdrawal is as easy as consent (statutory requirement) and its consequences are stated honestly ("you will stop receiving appointment reminders").
2. **Rights requests** raised by the patient, tracked with a reference number and a visible SLA clock:
   - **Access** — a summary of the personal data processed and with whom it has been shared (payers, labs, ABDM) → generated report.
   - **Correction / completion / updating** — e.g. spelling of a name, date of birth, contact; clinical corrections follow the amendment rule (an append-only correction with reason, never a silent overwrite of a clinical record).
   - **Erasure** — honoured except where retention is legally required (clinical and financial records under MRD retention, GST 8 years, medico-legal indefinite) — the response states the exact ground and what _was_ deleted (marketing profile, non-clinical uploads).
   - **Nomination** — appoint a nominee.
   - **Grievance** — routed to the Privacy Officer with an SLA; escalation to the Data Protection Board is explained if unresolved.
     Requests land in the DPO queue (EN-028), with a default **90-day statutory SLA** and a stricter internal target (default 30 days), reminders at 50 % and 80 % of the window, and an auditable closure with the response document.
3. **Access log transparency**: the patient can see who accessed their records (staff role and date, not personal identity, per policy) — a strong trust feature that also deters casual snooping.

### 3.10 Documents, uploads, feedback and support

- **Upload** (`portal.document_upload`): external reports, prescriptions, insurance documents, ID — tagged, virus-scanned, size-limited, visible to the care team and flagged as patient-provided (never merged into validated results).
- **Feedback** (EN-030): post-visit NPS/CSAT with the survey link, and public-review routing per the hospital's policy; **grievance** (NC-032) with a reference number and status; **ask a question** routed to the right desk with an SLA.
- **Notification preference centre**: per channel (SMS/WhatsApp/email/push) and per category (appointments, reports, bills, follow-ups, education, marketing), with quiet hours; service messages required for care are marked as such and cannot be silenced without a warning.

### 3.11 Sensitive records & privacy controls

- Categories the hospital designates sensitive (psychiatry OP-032, HIV/STI, fertility OP-024, oncology genetics, MLC, termination of pregnancy) follow a stricter rule: hidden by default from family/dependant viewers, optionally hidden from the patient's own default view behind a re-authentication step ("show sensitive records"), never included in shared links or family digests, and always excluded from any marketing segmentation.

### 3.12 Exceptions

- OTP not received → WhatsApp fallback, then voice OTP (EN-033), then desk-assisted verification.
- Multiple UHIDs / duplicate records → merge request to MRD with status visible to the patient.
- Deceased patient → account frozen; nominee/legal heir access follows a documented, verified process (never self-service).
- Payment succeeded but not reflected → status page with the gateway reference and an automatic reconciliation job; never a second charge.
- Report withheld (critical value, doctor hold) → an honest state message, not a blank screen.
- Offline (`portal.offline_pwa`) → cached dashboard, appointments, prescriptions and downloaded reports readable; actions queue with a clear "will send when online" state; payments never queue.

## 5. Business Rules & Validations

- **No record access without verified identity linkage.** A phone-number match alone creates a guest account with booking/payment only. Record access requires desk verification, ABHA match, or an OTP-consented family grant.
- **Family access is granted by the data subject, never by the requester.** An adult member consents on their own device; permissions are per-capability and revocable instantly; a revocation takes effect on the next request (no cached bypass).
- **Minors**: verifiable parental consent required; no behavioural tracking; no marketing; automatic prompt to transition at 18.
- **Release rules are server-side.** Critical/panic results are withheld from first delivery by portal (the clinical team calls first — EN-029/OP-004 rule); sensitive categories default to hidden from family; full notes require doctor authorisation or the configured interval; a withheld artefact shows an honest status, never a 404.
- **DPDP**: consent is itemised, purpose-limited, withdrawable in one action, and every state change is written to the EN-028 ledger with the notice version. Withdrawal of `service_comms` cannot silently break clinical safety notifications — the consequence is stated and critical clinical contact remains permissible on the legitimate-use ground.
- **DSAR SLA**: statutory clock (90 days default, configurable to a stricter jurisdictional requirement) starts at submission; reminders at 50 % and 80 %; a rejection must state the exact legal ground and what alternative was provided; erasure never deletes records required by MRD retention, GST, medico-legal or scheme rules, and says so explicitly.
- **Payments**: server-computed amounts only; idempotency keys on every payment; confirmation on gateway webhook only; no card data stored; refunds through the original instrument via EN-010.
- **Rate limits & abuse**: OTP per mobile per hour, login attempts, report downloads per hour, share-link creation; captcha on public endpoints; all enforced at EN-026.
- **No PHI in URLs, message bodies, push payloads or logs.** Share links are opaque tokens with expiry and view caps.
- **Deceased/frozen accounts**: no self-service access; nominee/legal-heir access follows a verified manual process with legal documents.
- **Accessibility is a requirement, not a nice-to-have**: WCAG 2.2 AA for every screen, keyboard operable, screen-reader labelled, contrast-checked, minimum tap target 44 px, works at 200 % zoom.
- **Language**: every patient-facing string is translated (en-IN, hi, ta, te, ml, kn, mr, bn + others as deployed); the DPDP notice must be available in the Eighth Schedule languages the hospital serves.
- **Offline** actions queue only where safe (draft feedback, form entry); never payments, never consent grants.

## 8. Screens (responsive web/PWA; phone-first, tablet and desktop layouts; shares components with OP-020)

- **Login** (phone/desktop): mobile field with country code, OTP entry with auto-read where supported, ABHA login button, language switcher, help link. Errors are human ("we could not send the code — try WhatsApp"). Accessibility: labelled inputs, 44 px targets, no timers that cannot be extended.

## 13. Non-functional

- **Volumes** (2000-bed group, 5,000 OP visits/day): 300k–600k registered accounts within two years, 30k–60k monthly active users, peak 2,000 concurrent sessions around report-release and appointment-opening times; 8k report views/day; 3k online payments/day.
- **Performance budgets**: TTI < 2.5 s on a 3G-class Android phone; LCP < 2.0 s; dashboard API p95 < 200 ms; timeline page (20 items) p95 < 300 ms; report PDF start-of-download < 1.5 s; queue status < 100 ms from a cached read model; bundle ≤ 200 KB gzipped for the first route with route-level code splitting.
- **Offline (`portal.offline_pwa`)**: service worker caches shell, dashboard, appointments, prescriptions and previously downloaded reports; queued actions limited to safe ones; explicit offline banner; background sync on reconnect.
- **Scale & resilience**: static shell on CDN; read models for dashboard/queue; rate limits per account and per IP; graceful degradation when a dependency (ABDM, gateway, PACS) is down, with a specific message naming what is unavailable.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.2 AA verified by automated (axe) and manual screen-reader testing on the golden paths; keyboard-only operation; 200 % zoom; captions on any video (PE-003); RPwD-aligned.
- **i18n**: full translation of every string, date/number formats per locale, `en-IN` currency (lakh/crore), Indic font loading optimised, RTL-ready.
- **Security**: OTP rate limiting and lockout, device binding, rotating refresh tokens in httpOnly cookies, CSP/HSTS/SRI, no PHI in URLs or push payloads, opaque expiring share tokens with view caps, virus scanning on uploads, per-request authorisation through the owning clinical modules, full PHI read audit (EN-024), annual pen-test on the portal surface, bot protection on public endpoints.
- **Printing**: reports, prescriptions, receipts and estimates print cleanly from the browser (print stylesheet) and via kiosk printers (EN-005).

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an unregistered mobile number, when the user logs in by OTP, then a guest account is created that can book and pay but shows no clinical records until identity is verified at the desk or by ABHA match.
2. Given a verified patient account, when a lab report is validated, then it appears in the timeline within 60 s (subject to the hold window) and a "report ready" message is sent without the result value in the message body.
3. Given a lab result flagged critical, then it is **not** released to the portal on validation; the portal shows "your doctor will contact you", and release occurs only after clinical acknowledgement.
4. Given a family invite to an adult, when the invitee consents by OTP on their own device with `view_records` off, then the inviter can book and pay but any record request returns 403 and is audited.
5. Given a family member revokes access, then the next record request fails immediately (no cached bypass) and the revocation is recorded with a timestamp.
6. Given a child profile, then it can only exist under a verified guardian account with recorded parental consent, no marketing message is ever sent to it, and at 18 the transition prompt appears.
7. Given the patient withdraws the `marketing` consent, then EN-028 records the withdrawal, NC-026 segmentation excludes them on the next run, and appointment reminders (service category) continue.
8. Given the patient raises an erasure request, then a reference number and SLA clock are shown, the DPO queue receives it, and the response states exactly what was erased and the legal ground for anything retained (MRD retention, GST 8 years, medico-legal).
9. Given a DSAR is open, then reminders fire at 50 % and 80 % of the SLA window, and the compliance report shows SLA adherence per request type.
10. Given the patient opens "who accessed my records", then the log shows role-level access entries for their own records only.
11. Given a payment is initiated and the gateway webhook does not arrive, then the payment shows `pending`, reconciliation resolves it, and no duplicate charge is possible (idempotency key test).
12. Given a scheme beneficiary, then the portal shows "cashless — you should not pay anything" with the helpline and offers no payment action for scheme-covered charges.
13. Given a psychiatry consultation record and a family member with `view_records` granted, then the sensitive record is not visible to the family and does not appear in any shared link or digest.
14. Given a report share link, then it is an opaque token, expires at the configured time, enforces the view cap, contains no PHI in the URL, and can be revoked by the patient.
15. Given the device is offline, then the dashboard, appointments and previously downloaded reports remain readable, the offline banner is shown, and payment actions are disabled rather than queued.
16. Given a 3G-class device, then the dashboard reaches TTI < 2.5 s and the first-route bundle is ≤ 200 KB gzipped (Lighthouse/k6 gate in CI).
17. Given screen-reader navigation of the golden paths (login, book, view report, pay), then all interactive elements are labelled, focus order is logical, and axe reports no critical violations.
18. Given the patient switches language to Tamil, then every patient-facing string, date and currency format changes, including the DPDP notice.
19. Given a deceased patient, then the account is frozen, self-service access is disabled, and nominee access requires the documented verified process.
20. Given 2,000 concurrent sessions during a report-release peak, then p95 dashboard API < 200 ms and no PHI appears in any log line (log-scanning test).
21. Given an ABDM consent is granted to another provider, then the consent artefact is recorded, visible in the portal with purpose and expiry, and revocable in one action.
22. Given a user without `portal.record.export`, when calling download-all, then 403 and an audit entry are recorded.

3. Which record categories are "sensitive" for your hospital, and what is the rule for family viewers?
4. Family access: do you allow record viewing by a family member with the patient's OTP consent only, or do you require desk verification? What evidence do you accept for guardianship of a dependant adult?

6. Do you want online payment for appointments (pay-before) or pay-at-hospital? What is the cancellation/refund policy and cut-off?
